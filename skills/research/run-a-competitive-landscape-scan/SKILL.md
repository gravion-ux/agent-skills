# Run a competitive landscape scan across categories, substitutes, and positioning

## Purpose

Run a competitive landscape scan across categories, substitutes, and positioning.

## When to use

Use when the user needs an up-to-date view of competitors and alternative offerings.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Define the market boundary, customer segment, and comparison categories.
2. Identify direct competitors, substitutes, and adjacent alternatives.
3. Collect current evidence on positioning, pricing, features, and traction.
4. Normalize comparisons to the same date and definition.
5. Summarise the landscape, white spaces, and changes since the last scan.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
