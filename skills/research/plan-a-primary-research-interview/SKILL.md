# Plan a primary research interview around hypotheses, probes, and decision use

## Purpose

Plan a primary research interview around hypotheses, probes, and decision use.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a rigorous interview plan for gathering original evidence.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Define the decision, target participant, and hypotheses the interview must test.
2. Write a timed guide with core questions and neutral follow-up probes.
3. Specify how each answer will be captured, coded, and compared.
4. Recruit for relevant expertise and contrasting perspectives.
5. Set consent, recording, privacy, and escalation safeguards.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
