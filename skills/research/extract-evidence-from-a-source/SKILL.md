# Extract evidence from a source with exact passages, context, and claim links

## Purpose

Extract evidence from a source with exact passages, context, and claim links.

## When to use

Use when the user needs precise support for a specific claim from a selected source.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Identify the passage that directly supports or challenges the target claim.
2. Record the exact quotation and its page, section, or timestamp.
3. Summarise the surrounding context, population, and method.
4. Link each extracted item to the claim it supports or weakens.
5. Flag ambiguity, missing context, and any uncertainty in the source.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
