# Maintain a citation ledger that links every claim to a dated, accessible source

## Purpose

Maintain a citation ledger that links every claim to a dated, accessible source.

## When to use

Use when the user needs repeatable citation tracking across a multi-source investigation.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Create a source ledger with stable identifiers, metadata, and access dates.
2. Link each claim to the ledger entries that support or challenge it.
3. Check that every factual claim has at least one traceable citation.
4. Flag broken links, undated sources, and claims supported only by summaries.
5. Export the ledger and unresolved citation gaps with the synthesis.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
