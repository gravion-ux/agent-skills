# Critique a study's method by testing fit, validity, and causal inference

## Purpose

Critique a study's method by testing fit, validity, and causal inference.

## When to use

Use when the user needs an evidence-based appraisal of a research study's methodology.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Extract the design, sampling frame, measures, analysis, and comparison strategy.
2. Check whether the sample and measures match the stated research question.
3. Identify confounding, selection, measurement, and attrition threats.
4. Test whether the analysis supports causal or generalisable conclusions.
5. Rank the limitations by likely impact and suggest feasible remedies.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
