# Write a research brief that connects evidence to a decision and confidence

## Purpose

Write a research brief that connects evidence to a decision and confidence.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a concise evidence briefing for a specific decision.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. State the decision, question, scope, date range, and allowed sources.
2. Summarise the strongest findings with linked primary sources.
3. Surface disagreements, evidence gaps, and confidence levels.
4. Translate the evidence into implications, options, and unresolved questions.
5. Attach the source ledger and name the next validation step.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
