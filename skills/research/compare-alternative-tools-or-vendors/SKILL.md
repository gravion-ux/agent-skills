# Compare alternative tools or vendors against shared requirements and total costs

## Purpose

Compare alternative tools or vendors against shared requirements and total costs.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a consistent vendor or tool comparison for a specific decision.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Translate the decision into weighted functional, risk, and cost requirements.
2. Collect current evidence from vendor materials, documentation, and independent sources.
3. Score each option against the same criteria and record evidence links.
4. Calculate total cost of ownership over the intended use period.
5. Recommend an option and state the assumptions and switching risks.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
