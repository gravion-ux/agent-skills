# Validate a claim with a primary source and preserve the evidence trail

## Purpose

Validate a claim with a primary source and preserve the evidence trail.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to verify a factual claim against original evidence.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Restate the claim as a testable statement with its key qualifiers.
2. Locate the original record, dataset, filing, study, or direct testimony.
3. Compare the primary source wording with every part of the claim.
4. Record dates, identifiers, direct excerpts, and any contradictions.
5. Issue a supported, contradicted, or unresolved verdict with confidence.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
