# Analyse interview transcripts by coding themes, contradictions, and supporting quotes

## Purpose

Analyse interview transcripts by coding themes, contradictions, and supporting quotes.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a defensible qualitative analysis of completed interview transcripts.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Create a clean transcript and remove identifiers before analysis.
2. Segment each transcript by topic and apply the agreed codebook.
3. Summarise themes across participants and retain representative quotations.
4. Test themes against contradictory cases and outlier accounts.
5. Write findings with evidence strength, limitations, and respondent counts.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
