# Spot dataset bias by examining coverage, measurement, and missingness patterns

## Purpose

Spot dataset bias by examining coverage, measurement, and missingness patterns.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to assess how dataset collection may distort findings.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Profile the population, sampling frame, and observation period in the dataset.
2. Compare represented and missing groups against relevant benchmarks.
3. Inspect measurement definitions for systematic differences between groups.
4. Check missingness, duplicates, and outliers for concentrated effects.
5. Document likely bias direction, severity, and any affected claims.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
