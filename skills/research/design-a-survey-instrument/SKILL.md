# Design a survey instrument that measures each construct without leading questions

## Purpose

Design a survey instrument that measures each construct without leading questions.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a valid and analysable questionnaire for a research study.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Map each research question to the constructs and observable behaviours to measure.
2. Write neutral questions with one idea and a consistent response scale.
3. Add screening, attention, and completeness checks where appropriate.
4. Pilot the instrument and revise confusing or double-barrelled items.
5. Document scoring rules, skip logic, and analysis thresholds.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
