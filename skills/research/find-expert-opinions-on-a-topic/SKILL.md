# Find expert opinions on a topic and separate informed views from promotion

## Purpose

Find expert opinions on a topic and separate informed views from promotion.

## When to use

Use when the user needs credible practitioner or academic perspectives on a subject.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Define the expertise or lived experience the topic requires.
2. Search named institutions, professional bodies, and verified expert profiles.
3. Collect direct statements with dates, roles, and source links.
4. Check whether each expert's view rests on evidence relevant to the question.
5. Group agreement, disagreement, and promotional statements in a sourced summary.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
