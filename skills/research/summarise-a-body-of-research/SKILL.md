# Summarise a body of research with agreements, disagreements, and confidence levels

## Purpose

Summarise a body of research with agreements, disagreements, and confidence levels.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a decision-ready synthesis across several research sources.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Group included sources by question, method, population, and evidence strength.
2. Extract each source's findings with a linked citation.
3. Identify recurring agreements, conflicts, and research gaps.
4. Assess which disagreements arise from methods, populations, or definitions.
5. State an overall conclusion with a confidence level and caveats.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
