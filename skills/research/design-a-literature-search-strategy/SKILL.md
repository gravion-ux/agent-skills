# Design a literature search strategy with databases, query terms, and inclusion criteria

## Purpose

Design a literature search strategy with databases, query terms, and inclusion criteria.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a reproducible search plan for finding relevant prior research.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Select databases and source types that cover the research question.
2. Build each search query from synonyms, controlled terms, and linked concepts.
3. Record inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, and the search date.
4. Add citation chaining and backward search for seminal references.
5. Pilot the strategy on a small sample and revise noisy terms.
6. Log every database, query, date, and result count.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
