# Scan preprint servers for recent work and label each result's review status

## Purpose

Scan preprint servers for recent work and label each result's review status.

## When to use

Use when the user needs an early view of new research that may not yet be peer reviewed.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Select preprint servers and subject categories that match the topic.
2. Run saved searches across titles, abstracts, and subject terms.
3. Record each result's identifier, date, version, and review status.
4. Screen for methodology, sample, and relevance to the research question.
5. Label unreviewed findings as preliminary and list records needing follow-up.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
