# Research a market size estimate by triangulating demand and supply evidence

## Purpose

Research a market size estimate by triangulating demand and supply evidence.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a defensible market size range for a defined segment.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a quick fact check with one authoritative source; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The question and why it matters
- The scope, the date range and the sources allowed
- The decision the research informs

## Procedure

1. Define the market, geography, customer segment, and measurement year.
2. Collect top-down and bottom-up inputs from dated, attributable sources.
3. Reconcile overlapping definitions and adjust for time and geography.
4. Calculate a range with explicit assumptions for each scenario.
5. Cross-check the estimate against comparable markets and disclose uncertainty.

## Output contract

A written synthesis with claims linked to sources, the disagreements surfaced and the confidence stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source
- [ ] Sources are dated and primary where possible
- [ ] Contradictions between sources are stated

## Safety notes

Never cite a source that was not opened, and never present an unverified claim as established.
