# Compare credit cards on the real cost of borrowing rather than on the headline rate

## Purpose

Compare credit cards on the real cost of borrowing rather than on the headline rate.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to choose between credit cards based on what they will actually pay.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. List the cards under consideration with their annual fee and purchase rate.
2. Write the cash advance rate and any foreign transaction or balance transfer fees.
3. Estimate the spend and balance the user would carry on each card.
4. Compute the total annual cost for each card from those two numbers.
5. Pick the cheapest on the estimate and note what would change that answer.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
