# Set up automated bill payments so bills leave on time without manual effort

## Purpose

Set up automated bill payments so bills leave on time without manual effort.

## When to use

Use when the user wants recurring bills to be paid automatically and on schedule.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. List each recurring bill with its amount, due date and the account it comes from.
2. Check each amount against the last three charges to catch anything that changed.
3. Set up a scheduled payment for each bill with a buffer before the due date.
4. Keep one account with enough balance to cover the first scheduled run.
5. Review the schedule after one cycle to confirm every payment landed on time.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
