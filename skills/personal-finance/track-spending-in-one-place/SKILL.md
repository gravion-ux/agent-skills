# Consolidate every spending source into one place so the totals can be trusted

## Purpose

Consolidate every spending source into one place so the totals can be trusted.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to see all of their spending together instead of scattered across accounts.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. List every account, card and cash source the user spends from.
2. Choose one place to record transactions, either a spreadsheet or an app.
3. Import or enter the transactions from each source into that one place.
4. Agree the categories and map any source-specific label to them.
5. Reconcile the total against a bank statement once to confirm accuracy.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
