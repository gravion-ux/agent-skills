# Set up an emergency fund sized to the real cost of losing income for several months

## Purpose

Set up an emergency fund sized to the real cost of losing income for several months.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a buffer to cover unexpected costs or a sudden loss of income.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. Calculate the essential monthly costs from the budget, not the average spend.
2. Multiply by the number of months the user wants to cover.
3. List the current savings that already count toward that target.
4. Open a separate accessible account that keeps the buffer out of daily spending.
5. Automate a fixed transfer on payday until the target is reached.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
