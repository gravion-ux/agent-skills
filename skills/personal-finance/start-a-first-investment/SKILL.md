# Start a first investment with a clear rationale, a small stake and a stated time horizon

## Purpose

Start a first investment with a clear rationale, a small stake and a stated time horizon.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to make their first investment and needs a safe starting point.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. State the goal the investment serves and the horizon it needs to cover.
2. Confirm an emergency buffer is in place before committing any money.
3. Choose a diversified, low-cost option appropriate to the stated horizon.
4. Invest a small fixed amount that the user can afford to leave untouched.
5. Set a review date rather than reacting to short-term price moves.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
