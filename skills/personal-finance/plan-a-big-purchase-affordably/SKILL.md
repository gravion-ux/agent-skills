# Plan a large purchase so it fits the budget without crowding out the savings

## Purpose

Plan a large purchase so it fits the budget without crowding out the savings.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to buy something expensive without overextending their money.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. State the total price including tax, delivery, insurance and setup costs.
2. Check the amount against the savings available and the monthly budget headroom.
3. Split the purchase into a deposit, a balance and the month each is due.
4. Show what the monthly payment would do to the savings target and the buffer.
5. Set the decision date and the conditions under which the purchase is confirmed.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
