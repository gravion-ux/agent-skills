# Pay down debt with an ordered plan that targets the highest cost first

## Purpose

Pay down debt with an ordered plan that targets the highest cost first.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a concrete schedule for clearing credit card or other debts.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. List every debt with its balance, interest rate and minimum payment.
2. Rank the debts by rate, highest first, and confirm the required minimums are affordable.
3. Direct every spare amount to the top-ranked debt beyond its minimum.
4. Set a monthly payment amount and a target date for clearing that debt.
5. Roll the freed payment into the next debt once the first is cleared.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
