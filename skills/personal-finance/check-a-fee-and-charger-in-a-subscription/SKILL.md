# Check a subscription for hidden fees, price rises and charges that no longer apply

## Purpose

Check a subscription for hidden fees, price rises and charges that no longer apply.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to audit a subscription before deciding to keep it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. Find the current plan price and every fee the provider lists for it.
2. Check whether the price has risen recently or rises at renewal.
3. Identify any add-ons, premium tiers or charges that were added separately.
4. Confirm what happens to the price if the subscription is cancelled.
5. Decide keep, downgrade or cancel, and note the renewal date to revisit it.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
