# Plan a sinking fund for a known future cost instead of paying for it from monthly income

## Purpose

Plan a sinking fund for a known future cost instead of paying for it from monthly income.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to save steadily for a specific planned purchase or renewal.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for company accounting or tax filing; use finance-accounting instead.

## Inputs required

- The income, the bills and the goals
- The time horizon and the risk tolerance
- The jurisdiction for tax and benefit rules

## Procedure

1. Name the target cost, the target date and the amount involved.
2. Divide the amount by the number of months until the target date.
3. Open a separate account named for that goal so the money is not spent elsewhere.
4. Automate the monthly contribution on the same day each month.
5. Review the balance a month before the target date to confirm it is on track.

## Output contract

A prioritised plan with a budget breakdown, an emergency buffer target and the next concrete action.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan balances on the real numbers
- [ ] High-interest debt is addressed first if it exists
- [ ] Tax or benefit claims are flagged for verification

## Safety notes

Never give personalised tax or investment advice as if it were professional advice, and never ask for account numbers or credentials.
