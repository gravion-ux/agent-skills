# Prepare for a hard conversation with someone close so it ends in clarity rather than damage

## Purpose

Prepare for a hard conversation with someone close so it ends in clarity rather than damage.

## When to use

Use when the user must raise something difficult with a friend, partner or family member.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Write the one point the conversation actually needs to resolve.
2. Separate the facts from the interpretation and keep only the facts.
3. Rehearse the opening two sentences, because the start sets the tone for the rest.
4. Decide what the user is willing to change and what they are not.
5. Plan how to end the conversation, including when to pause and continue later.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
