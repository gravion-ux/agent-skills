# Make a new friend as an adult through repeated low-pressure contact

## Purpose

Make a new friend as an adult through repeated low-pressure contact.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to build a new friendship and is unsure how to start as an adult.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Choose a setting where the same people return regularly, such as a class or a club.
2. Talk to one person briefly about something the setting makes obvious.
3. Suggest a low-commitment second meeting such as a coffee or a walk.
4. Keep the conversation going by asking about the thing the other person cares about.
5. Let the relationship grow through repeated contact rather than trying to fast-track it.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
