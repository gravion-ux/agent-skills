# Decline a social invitation warmly, briefly and without inventing an excuse

## Purpose

Decline a social invitation warmly, briefly and without inventing an excuse.

## When to use

Use when the user cannot attend an event and wants to decline without damaging the relationship.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Reply promptly, even if the answer is still being decided.
2. Decline directly in the first sentence rather than leaving it ambiguous.
3. Give a brief reason only if one is genuinely needed, and keep it short.
4. Offer an alternative way to connect if the relationship matters.
5. Send a short warm message afterwards so the decline does not feel cold.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
