# Support a friend in crisis by staying present, listening well and connecting them to help

## Purpose

Support a friend in crisis by staying present, listening well and connecting them to help.

## When to use

Use when the user is worried about a friend who is in emotional or mental distress.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Contact the friend soon and keep the contact regular rather than waiting for them to reply.
2. Listen without fixing, arguing or offering solutions the friend did not ask for.
3. Ask plainly and directly whether they are thinking about harming themselves.
4. Help them reach professional support and offer to go with them or make the call.
5. Agree a check-in plan and involve another trusted person if the risk seems high.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
