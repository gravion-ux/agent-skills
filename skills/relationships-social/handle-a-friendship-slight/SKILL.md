# Handle a small hurt in a friendship by naming it once and checking what it meant

## Purpose

Handle a small hurt in a friendship by naming it once and checking what it meant.

## When to use

Use when the user feels let down or overlooked by a friend and is deciding whether to raise it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Decide whether the event matters enough to raise or should simply be let go.
2. Describe the specific action that felt wrong, without characterising the friend's motives.
3. Ask what the friend saw the situation as, because the gap is usually there.
4. Agree what each side will do differently if the same situation recurs.
5. Keep the exchange short and do not reopen every past instance.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
