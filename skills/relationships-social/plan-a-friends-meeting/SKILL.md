# Plan a friends meeting that fits everyone's time and ends with a reason to meet again

## Purpose

Plan a friends meeting that fits everyone's time and ends with a reason to meet again.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to organise a get-together that works for a group of friends.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Pick a date range and a place rather than a single fixed option.
2. Ask each person for their constraint directly instead of inferring it.
3. Choose the option that most people can reach with the least effort.
4. Set a start and an end time so the meeting does not stretch indefinitely.
5. Agree the next meet-up before the current one ends.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
