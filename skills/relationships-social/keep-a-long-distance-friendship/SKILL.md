# Keep a long-distance friendship alive with small, regular contact instead of rare long calls

## Purpose

Keep a long-distance friendship alive with small, regular contact instead of rare long calls.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to maintain a friendship across distance or a busy period.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Agree a contact rhythm that both sides can keep without it feeling like an obligation.
2. Mix short, frequent messages with occasional longer conversations.
3. Send something specific from the other person's life rather than only reacting to their posts.
4. Plan the next visit or shared event early so distance has a visible end point.
5. Let the rhythm change when life changes, and set a new one rather than letting it fade.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
