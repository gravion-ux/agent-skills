# Plan a lesson a substitute can teach without knowing the class, with the class in order

## Purpose

Plan a lesson a substitute can teach without knowing the class, with the class in order.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to leave a lesson for someone who does not know the students.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for self-directed study; use learning-skills instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner group and their level
- The learning objectives
- The session format and the time available

## Procedure

1. Write the objective and the timings at the top, in the order the substitute will need them.
2. List the materials and where they are, and leave them ready in the room.
3. Provide the starter activity exactly, including the answers if there are any.
4. Include what to do with a class that finishes early and what to do with one that struggles.
5. Write the names of the students who need specific attention and the behaviour notes.

## Output contract

A lesson plan with objectives, activities, materials and an assessment.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every objective has an activity and an assessment
- [ ] The difficulty matches the learner level
- [ ] Materials are ready before the session

## Safety notes

Never use copyrighted source material without permission, and never grade a person without stating the rubric.
