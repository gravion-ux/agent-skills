# Plan an outdoor lesson with the risk assessment, the logistics and a fallback done first

## Purpose

Plan an outdoor lesson with the risk assessment, the logistics and a fallback done first.

## When to use

Use when the user is taking a class or a group outside for a lesson.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for self-directed study; use learning-skills instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner group and their level
- The learning objectives
- The session format and the time available

## Procedure

1. Check the site, the access, the toilets and the shelter available.
2. Write the risk assessment with the hazards and who is responsible for each group.
3. Plan the headcount procedure at the start, at the move and at the end.
4. Put the instructions and the timings where the group can hear them without a phone.
5. Write the plan for the weather and the plan for a group not arriving.

## Output contract

A lesson plan with objectives, activities, materials and an assessment.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every objective has an activity and an assessment
- [ ] The difficulty matches the learner level
- [ ] Materials are ready before the session

## Safety notes

Never use copyrighted source material without permission, and never grade a person without stating the rubric.
