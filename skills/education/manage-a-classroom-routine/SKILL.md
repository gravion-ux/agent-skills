# Manage a classroom routine with a short signal that works the first time it is used

## Purpose

Manage a classroom routine with a short signal that works the first time it is used.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a routine that keeps a class moving without constant correction.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for self-directed study; use learning-skills instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner group and their level
- The learning objectives
- The session format and the time available

## Procedure

1. Choose the signal for when attention is needed and practise it with the class.
2. State the routine in the order the steps happen.
3. Make the routine visible, with the steps written where they can be seen.
4. Agree what happens when the routine is followed and what happens when it is not.
5. Review the routine after a week and change the part that is not working.

## Output contract

A lesson plan with objectives, activities, materials and an assessment.

- [ ] Every objective has an activity and an assessment
- [ ] The difficulty matches the learner level
- [ ] Materials are ready before the session
