# Review a course with a student by asking what worked, what did not and what to change

## Purpose

Review a course with a student by asking what worked, what did not and what to change.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to gather honest feedback from a learner about a course.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for self-directed study; use learning-skills instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner group and their level
- The learning objectives
- The session format and the time available

## Procedure

1. Ask the student to name the most useful session and the least useful one.
2. Ask what they still do not understand, in their own words.
3. Ask which parts they did not have time for and why.
4. Ask what they would change if they ran the course themselves.
5. Write the feedback down as specific requests rather than as general ratings.

## Output contract

A lesson plan with objectives, activities, materials and an assessment.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every objective has an activity and an assessment
- [ ] The difficulty matches the learner level
- [ ] Materials are ready before the session

## Safety notes

Never use copyrighted source material without permission, and never grade a person without stating the rubric.
