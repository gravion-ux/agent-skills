# Adapt a lesson for remote delivery by changing the interaction, not just the setting

## Purpose

Adapt a lesson for remote delivery by changing the interaction, not just the setting.

## When to use

Use when the user has to teach an existing lesson plan to learners who are not in the room.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for self-directed study; use learning-skills instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner group and their level
- The learning objectives
- The session format and the time available

## Procedure

1. Identify the parts of the lesson that depend on the teacher seeing everyone.
2. Replace the group work with pairs that check in on a shared channel.
3. Build in a check that every learner has responded, not only the confident ones.
4. Make the materials available before the session and keep the screen simple.
5. Plan a follow-up for the learners who could not connect or fell behind.

## Output contract

A lesson plan with objectives, activities, materials and an assessment.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every objective has an activity and an assessment
- [ ] The difficulty matches the learner level
- [ ] Materials are ready before the session

## Safety notes

Never use copyrighted source material without permission, and never grade a person without stating the rubric.
