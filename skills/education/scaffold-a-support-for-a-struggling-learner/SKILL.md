# Support a struggling learner by reducing the gap one step at a time instead of taking over

## Purpose

Support a struggling learner by reducing the gap one step at a time instead of taking over.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to help a learner who keeps getting stuck on a task.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for self-directed study; use learning-skills instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner group and their level
- The learning objectives
- The session format and the time available

## Procedure

1. Find the exact point where the learner's understanding stops, not where the answer is wrong.
2. Break the task into smaller steps with a check after each one.
3. Model the first step explicitly and then fade the support.
4. Give a shorter version of the task with the same success criteria.
5. Agree when the support will be reduced and how progress will be checked.

## Output contract

A lesson plan with objectives, activities, materials and an assessment.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every objective has an activity and an assessment
- [ ] The difficulty matches the learner level
- [ ] Materials are ready before the session

## Safety notes

Never use copyrighted source material without permission, and never grade a person without stating the rubric.
