# Build a formative assessment that shows what learners can do right now

## Purpose

Build a formative assessment that shows what learners can do right now.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a quick check of understanding during or shortly after teaching.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for self-directed study; use learning-skills instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner group and their level
- The learning objectives
- The session format and the time available

## Procedure

1. Choose two or three objectives to check and leave the rest alone.
2. Write items that each map to one checked objective.
3. Include at least one item with a wrong answer likely to reveal a specific misconception.
4. Define the score that means the objective has been met.
5. Write the action to take for each score, before the assessment is given.

## Output contract

A lesson plan with objectives, activities, materials and an assessment.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every objective has an activity and an assessment
- [ ] The difficulty matches the learner level
- [ ] Materials are ready before the session

## Safety notes

Never use copyrighted source material without permission, and never grade a person without stating the rubric.
