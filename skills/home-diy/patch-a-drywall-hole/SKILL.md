# Patch a hole in drywall so the repair blends into the surrounding wall

## Purpose

Patch a hole in drywall so the repair blends into the surrounding wall.

## When to use

Use when the user has a hole or a dent in a plasterboard wall to repair.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Clear away loose paper and dust from around the hole.
2. Cut a backing patch slightly larger than the hole and fix it behind the wall.
3. Apply filler over the patch and the edges, letting it dry completely.
4. Sand the filler smooth with fine paper and check the repair under angled light.
5. Prime the repair and repaint the area with a feathered edge.
