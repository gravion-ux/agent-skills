# Dispose of old household items safely by finding the right route for each material

## Purpose

Dispose of old household items safely by finding the right route for each material.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to get rid of old furniture, appliances or electronics.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Separate the items by material, separating electronics, chemicals and textiles.
2. Check the local council or waste service rules for each category before moving anything.
3. Take electronics to an approved recycling point rather than the general waste.
4. Arrange collection for bulky items instead of leaving them for the normal collection.
5. Confirm the items are gone and keep the receipt for anything chargeable.

## Output contract

A step-by-step plan with the tools, materials, timings and the finish to expect.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The shut-off and safety steps come first
- [ ] Materials match the existing system
- [ ] The user is told when to stop and call a professional

## Safety notes

Never advise on live electrical or gas work without a qualified professional, and always state how to shut the water or power off.
