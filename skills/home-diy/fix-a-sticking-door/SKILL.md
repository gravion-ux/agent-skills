# Fix a sticking door by finding where it binds and correcting that point first

## Purpose

Fix a sticking door by finding where it binds and correcting that point first.

## When to use

Use when the user has a door that sticks, catches or takes force to close.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Work out whether the door binds at the top, the bottom or the latch side.
2. Check the hinge screws first, because a loose hinge causes most sticking doors.
3. Sand the edge that is rubbing and test the door after each pass.
4. Trim the strike plate or the latch only if the hinge fix did not solve it.
5. Oil the hinges, close the door and confirm it latches without force.
