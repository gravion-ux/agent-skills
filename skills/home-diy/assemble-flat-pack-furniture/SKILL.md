# Assemble flat-pack furniture in the right order with the fittings checked before it is built

## Purpose

Assemble flat-pack furniture in the right order with the fittings checked before it is built.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to put together a flat-pack piece of furniture.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Unpack everything and check the parts against the inventory before starting.
2. Find every fitting, including the ones in the small bag, and count them.
3. Assemble the frame on a flat surface with the fittings only hand-tight at first.
4. Square the frame with a diagonal measurement before tightening anything fully.
5. Tighten every fixing in the order the instructions give, then check the frame is square again.

## Output contract

A step-by-step plan with the tools, materials, timings and the finish to expect.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The shut-off and safety steps come first
- [ ] Materials match the existing system
- [ ] The user is told when to stop and call a professional

## Safety notes

Never advise on live electrical or gas work without a qualified professional, and always state how to shut the water or power off.
