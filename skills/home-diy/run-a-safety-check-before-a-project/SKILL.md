# Run a safety check before starting a project so the hazards are identified in advance

## Purpose

Run a safety check before starting a project so the hazards are identified in advance.

## When to use

Use when the user is about to begin a home project and wants to check it is safe to proceed.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. List the hazards for the job, including power, water, gas and working at height.
2. Locate and label the shut-offs for the services the job touches.
3. Check the area is clear of pets, children and trip hazards.
4. Inspect the tools and the protective equipment before starting.
5. Stop and call a professional if the job needs live electrical work or structural alteration.

## Output contract

A step-by-step plan with the tools, materials, timings and the finish to expect.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The shut-off and safety steps come first
- [ ] Materials match the existing system
- [ ] The user is told when to stop and call a professional

## Safety notes

Never advise on live electrical or gas work without a qualified professional, and always state how to shut the water or power off.
