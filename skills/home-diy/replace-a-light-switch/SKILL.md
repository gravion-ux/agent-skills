# Replace a light switch by isolating the circuit and matching the existing wiring type

## Purpose

Replace a light switch by isolating the circuit and matching the existing wiring type.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to swap a broken or outdated light switch for a new one.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Switch off the circuit at the consumer unit and confirm the power with a tester.
2. Photograph the existing wiring and note which conductor is the live.
3. Check whether the existing box has a earth conductor and enough space for the new switch.
4. Match the replacement to the existing wiring arrangement and fit it into the box.
5. Restore the power and test the switch in both positions before refitting the plate.

A step-by-step plan with the tools, materials, timings and the finish to expect.
