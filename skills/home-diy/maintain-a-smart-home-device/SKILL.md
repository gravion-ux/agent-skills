# Maintain a smart home device by checking its power, connection, firmware and settings

## Purpose

Maintain a smart home device by checking its power, connection, firmware and settings.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a smart home device to keep working reliably.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Confirm the device still has power and that its hub or network is reachable.
2. Check the app for a firmware update and install it when the device is on mains power.
3. Confirm the automation and schedules still point at the right devices.
4. Check the battery level or the sensor reading if the device reports oddly.
5. Write down the reset procedure so the device can be recovered without guesswork.

## Output contract

A step-by-step plan with the tools, materials, timings and the finish to expect.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The shut-off and safety steps come first
- [ ] Materials match the existing system
- [ ] The user is told when to stop and call a professional

## Safety notes

Never advise on live electrical or gas work without a qualified professional, and always state how to shut the water or power off.
