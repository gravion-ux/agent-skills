# Unblock a slow drain by working from the least invasive method upwards

## Purpose

Unblock a slow drain by working from the least invasive method upwards.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to clear a blocked sink, shower or toilet drain.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Stop the water flow into the drain and put a bucket and towel underneath.
2. Remove the grate or stopper and check for a blockage just below the surface.
3. Use a plunger matched to the drain size with enough water to cover the cup.
4. Try a drain snake or a bent wire if the plunger does not move it.
5. Check for a cause further down, such as a trap or a blocked shared run, before calling a plumber.
