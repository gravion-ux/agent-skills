# Paint a room evenly by preparing the surface, cutting in first and keeping the paint wet

## Purpose

Paint a room evenly by preparing the surface, cutting in first and keeping the paint wet.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to repaint a room properly and get a clean finish.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Move the furniture out and protect anything that stays with dust sheets.
2. Fill the holes, sand the repairs and wipe the walls down before painting.
3. Cut in around the edges and corners with a brush before using the roller.
4. Roll in a W pattern and keep a wet edge so the paint does not dry mid-wall.
5. Let the first coat dry fully, then apply a second coat in the same order.
