# Seal a drafty window by closing the gap with the right material for the frame type

## Purpose

Seal a drafty window by closing the gap with the right material for the frame type.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to stop draughts and heat loss around a window.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Identify the frame material and whether the window opens, because the fix differs.
2. Close the window and look along the edges to find where the draught enters.
3. Choose self-adhesive seal strip for the gap type and the frame material.
4. Clean and dry the surface thoroughly before applying the strip.
5. Test the window closes and latches as it did before the strip was applied.
