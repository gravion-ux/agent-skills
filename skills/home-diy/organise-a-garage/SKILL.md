# Organise a garage by sorting what is kept, what goes and where each item lives

## Purpose

Organise a garage by sorting what is kept, what goes and where each item lives.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to clear and organise a garage or storage space.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Empty the space completely and sort everything into keep, donate, sell and dispose piles.
2. Discard the broken and the duplicated items first so the volume drops before anything is placed.
3. Group the remaining items by how often they are used rather than by type.
4. Label the shelves and the boxes so a returning item has an obvious home.
5. Keep the floor and the access paths clear and put the most used items at waist height.

## Output contract

A step-by-step plan with the tools, materials, timings and the finish to expect.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The shut-off and safety steps come first
- [ ] Materials match the existing system
- [ ] The user is told when to stop and call a professional

## Safety notes

Never advise on live electrical or gas work without a qualified professional, and always state how to shut the water or power off.
