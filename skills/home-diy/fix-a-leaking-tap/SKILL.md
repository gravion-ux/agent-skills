# Fix a leaking tap by identifying whether the leak is at the handle or the spout

## Purpose

Fix a leaking tap by identifying whether the leak is at the handle or the spout.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to stop a tap that drips or leaks at the handle.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Shut off the water at the isolation valve under the sink and confirm the flow has stopped.
2. Open the tap to drain the pressure and place a towel over the drain.
3. Check whether the leak comes from the handle, the spout or the base of the tap.
4. Replace the washer or the cartridge for that specific fault.
5. Turn the water back on slowly and check the repair under the sink for drips.
