# Hang a shelf level by finding solid fixings and checking the level before drilling

## Purpose

Hang a shelf level by finding solid fixings and checking the level before drilling.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to put a shelf or a wall-mounted item on a wall.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Find the studs or the solid masonry with a detector and mark their positions.
2. Mark the shelf line with a spirit level held against the wall.
3. Choose fixings that match the wall type and the weight being carried.
4. Drill the pilot holes at the marked points and check the level once more.
5. Fix the shelf, load it gradually and recheck that it is still level.
