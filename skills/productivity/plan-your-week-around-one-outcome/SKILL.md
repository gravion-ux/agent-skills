# Choose one weekly outcome and schedule the work that can actually produce it

## Purpose

Choose one weekly outcome and schedule the work that can actually produce it.

## When to use

Use when the user wants the coming week organised around a single meaningful result.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a shared team workflow; use project-management instead.

## Inputs required

- The tasks or commitments in scope
- The time and attention available
- The deadline or habit being targeted

## Procedure

1. Name the one outcome the week must deliver and write it as a done condition.
2. List the tasks that could contribute to that outcome.
3. Cut the tasks that serve a different goal and move them to a later week.
4. Schedule the largest remaining block of effort early in the week.
5. Add a mid-week check on the outcome's progress.
6. Protect a rest period that does not depend on the outcome being met.

## Output contract

A prioritised, time-boxed plan with the next concrete action stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The first action can start immediately
- [ ] The plan fits the available time
- [ ] Each item has an explicit done condition

## Safety notes

Never schedule rest, health or recovery time away to fit more work in.
