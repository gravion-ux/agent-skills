# Audit calendar load and return reclaimed hours to focused work with explicit cuts

## Purpose

Audit calendar load and return reclaimed hours to focused work with explicit cuts.

## When to use

Use when the user finds too many meetings crowding out their own work.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a shared team workflow; use project-management instead.

## Inputs required

- The tasks or commitments in scope
- The time and attention available
- The deadline or habit being targeted

## Procedure

1. List every recurring and one-off meeting from the past two weeks.
2. Compute the total hours and the share spent on internal recurring meetings.
3. Cancel or shorten the meetings that duplicate information available in writing.
4. Propose agenda-free defaults, such as 25-minute slots, for the remaining meetings.
5. Block the reclaimed hours for focused work before the next meeting starts.

## Output contract

A prioritised, time-boxed plan with the next concrete action stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The first action can start immediately
- [ ] The plan fits the available time
- [ ] Each item has an explicit done condition

## Safety notes

Never schedule rest, health or recovery time away to fit more work in.
