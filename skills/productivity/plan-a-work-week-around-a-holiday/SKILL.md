# Fit working days around a holiday and shift deadlines before the gap arrives

## Purpose

Fit working days around a holiday and shift deadlines before the gap arrives.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a working plan that accounts for a holiday or short break.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a shared team workflow; use project-management instead.

## Inputs required

- The tasks or commitments in scope
- The time and attention available
- The deadline or habit being targeted

## Procedure

1. Mark the holiday and the working days left on either side of it.
2. List the deadlines that fall during or immediately after the break.
3. Move each deadline earlier and confirm the change with its owner.
4. Schedule the urgent items on the working days before the break.
5. Reserve one pre-break day for handover, so nothing waits across the gap.
6. Keep the return-to-work morning free of new commitments.

## Output contract

A prioritised, time-boxed plan with the next concrete action stated.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The first action can start immediately
- [ ] The plan fits the available time
- [ ] Each item has an explicit done condition

## Safety notes

Never schedule rest, health or recovery time away to fit more work in.
