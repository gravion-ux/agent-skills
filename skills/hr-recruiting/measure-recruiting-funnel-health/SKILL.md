# Measure recruiting funnel health from stage conversion, time in stage and drop-off reasons

## Purpose

Measure recruiting funnel health from stage conversion, time in stage and drop-off reasons.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to know where a hiring process is losing candidates.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Count the candidates entering and leaving each stage of the funnel.
2. Calculate the conversion rate and the median time in stage for each step.
3. Record the reason for every drop-off, especially the ones at the final stage.
4. Compare the stages against the target and identify the largest gap.
5. Propose one change to the worst stage and set a date to measure it again.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
