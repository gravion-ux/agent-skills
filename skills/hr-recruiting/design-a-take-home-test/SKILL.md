# Design a take-home test that mirrors the real work and fits the candidate's time

## Purpose

Design a take-home test that mirrors the real work and fits the candidate's time.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to assess a candidate's skills with a practical exercise.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Choose a task that is a realistic slice of the actual role rather than a puzzle.
2. Set the time limit and state it clearly, with what the candidate may or may not use.
3. Give the inputs, the expected output and the acceptance criteria.
4. Make the work small enough that a strong candidate finishes and a weak one does not.
5. Agree the scoring rubric before the exercise goes out.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
