# Offboard an employee by securing access, recovering property and recording the end cleanly

## Purpose

Offboard an employee by securing access, recovering property and recording the end cleanly.

## When to use

Use when the user is handling a resignation or a departure.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Confirm the leaving date, the notice and the reason in writing with the right people.
2. Schedule the access removal for the agreed time rather than immediately.
3. Recover the equipment, the access card and the company property with a signed hand-back.
4. Gather the handover notes, the open work and the outstanding approvals.
5. Close the payroll, the benefits and the accounts, and keep the record for the retention period.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
