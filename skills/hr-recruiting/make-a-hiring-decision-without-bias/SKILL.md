# Make a hiring decision by applying the agreed criteria to the evidence, with no gut override

## Purpose

Make a hiring decision by applying the agreed criteria to the evidence, with no gut override.

## When to use

Use when the user has to choose between candidates and is worried about bias.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Compare the evidence for each candidate against each criterion in the same order.
2. Check whether any criterion is being applied more strictly for one candidate than another.
3. Separate the decisions where the evidence is thin from the ones where it is clear.
4. Have another interviewer independently score the finalists before the final decision.
5. Record the decision and the evidence, and note any criteria the process failed to test.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
