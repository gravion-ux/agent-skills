# Write an offer letter that states the terms plainly and protects both sides

## Purpose

Write an offer letter that states the terms plainly and protects both sides.

## When to use

Use when the user has agreed a package and needs to put the offer in writing.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. State the role, the start date, the location and the working pattern.
2. List the base pay, the variable elements and the benefits with their conditions.
3. Include the probation period, the notice period and the contingencies of the offer.
4. Add the signature blocks and the deadline for accepting.
5. Have the final version checked against the approved terms before it is sent.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
