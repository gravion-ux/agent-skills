# Write a job description that states the outcomes, the work and the requirements clearly

## Purpose

Write a job description that states the outcomes, the work and the requirements clearly.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to open a role and needs a job description to publish.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Write the three outcomes the person will own in their first year.
2. Describe the actual day-to-day work rather than the team structure.
3. List the requirements as evidence of ability, such as years or equivalent experience.
4. State the location, the working pattern and the compensation range honestly.
5. Remove every requirement that is not clearly tied to one of the outcomes.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
