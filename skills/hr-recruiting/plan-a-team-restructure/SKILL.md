# Plan a team restructure around the work that must be done and the people affected

## Purpose

Plan a team restructure around the work that must be done and the people affected.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to change the shape or the roles of a team.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. State what the new structure must achieve that the current one does not.
2. Map the work to the roles and identify what genuinely changes.
3. Check the options against the constraints, such as location, budget and contracts.
4. Plan the communication, the timeline and the consultation that the change requires.
5. Define what support each affected person receives and what the first ninety days look like.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
