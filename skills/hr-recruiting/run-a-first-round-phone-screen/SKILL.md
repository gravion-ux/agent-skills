# Run a first phone screen that confirms the basics and holds the candidate's interest

## Purpose

Run a first phone screen that confirms the basics and holds the candidate's interest.

## When to use

Use when the user is conducting the first conversation with a candidate.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Set the agenda and the length in the invitation so the candidate knows what is coming.
2. Open with the context on the role and why the company is talking to them.
3. Ask about the most relevant recent experience before asking anything theoretical.
4. Check the practical facts, such as location, notice period and salary expectation.
5. Close with what happens next and when the candidate will hear from you.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
