# Write candidate feedback that is specific, evidence-based and safe to share

## Purpose

Write candidate feedback that is specific, evidence-based and safe to share.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to write the feedback that accompanies a hiring decision.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Write the evidence observed against each criterion, quoting or referencing what was said.
2. Separate the observation from the interpretation of it.
3. State the decision and the reasoning that connects the evidence to it.
4. Avoid any reference to characteristics that are not relevant to the role.
5. Write it so it could be read to the candidate without changing its meaning.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
