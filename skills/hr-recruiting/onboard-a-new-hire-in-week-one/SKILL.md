# Onboard a new hire in week one with access, context and a clear first deliverable

## Purpose

Onboard a new hire in week one with access, context and a clear first deliverable.

## When to use

Use when the user is preparing for a new starter joining the team.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Request the accounts, the permissions and the equipment before the start date.
2. Give them a written first week with the people they meet and what each meeting is for.
3. Name one small, real deliverable they can finish in the first week.
4. Arrange the team introductions and the context session with the hiring manager.
5. Check in at the end of the week and fix anything that blocked them on day one.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
