# Negotiate an offer by clarifying what the candidate wants and staying inside the approved band

## Purpose

Negotiate an offer by clarifying what the candidate wants and staying inside the approved band.

## When to use

Use when the user is discussing package details with a candidate who has an offer.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Know the approved band, the level and what the budget can still move for.
2. Ask the candidate what matters most to them rather than guessing.
3. Trade on the whole package, such as start date, equity or holiday, not salary alone.
4. Get any revised band approved before it is offered in writing.
5. Record the agreed package and confirm it in a written offer.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
