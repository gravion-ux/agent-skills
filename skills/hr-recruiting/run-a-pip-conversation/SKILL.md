# Run a performance improvement conversation with specific expectations, support and a review date

## Purpose

Run a performance improvement conversation with specific expectations, support and a review date.

## When to use

Use when the user has to tell an employee their performance is below what the role requires.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Confirm the expectations were communicated before, and find the written evidence.
2. State the gap between the expectation and the observed performance, with examples.
3. Listen to the explanation and check whether anything was blocking the performance.
4. Agree the specific improvements, the support provided and the review date.
5. Document the conversation and the agreed plan, and share it with the right people.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
