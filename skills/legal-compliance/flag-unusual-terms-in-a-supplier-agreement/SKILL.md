# Flag unusual terms in a supplier agreement by comparing them against the standard position

## Purpose

Flag unusual terms in a supplier agreement by comparing them against the standard position.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to know which terms in a supplier agreement depart from normal.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. List the terms that materially shift cost, liability or control between the parties.
2. Compare each one against the organisation's standard supplier terms.
3. Check the terms that are hard to reverse, such as auto-renewal, exclusivity and audit rights.
4. Check the terms that are easy to miss, such as the liability cap and the termination notice period.
5. Rank the deviations by commercial risk and write the requested change for each.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
