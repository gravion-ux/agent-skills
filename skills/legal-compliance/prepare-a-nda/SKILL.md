# Prepare an NDA with the narrowest workable confidentiality terms for the discussion at hand

## Purpose

Prepare an NDA with the narrowest workable confidentiality terms for the discussion at hand.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a non-disclosure agreement for a conversation or a potential deal.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. Write what is actually being shared so the definition of confidential information is specific.
2. Set the term to match how long the information stays sensitive.
3. List the permitted recipients and the exclusions that apply to them.
4. Add the return or destruction obligation and any carve-outs the law requires.
5. Have a qualified reviewer check the jurisdiction and the remedy clauses before it is signed.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
