# Map privacy obligations to the specific controls that satisfy them

## Purpose

Map privacy obligations to the specific controls that satisfy them.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to connect a privacy obligation to the technical or organisational control in place.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. List each privacy obligation with its source and the data it applies to.
2. For each one, name the control that satisfies it and where that control is evidenced.
3. Mark the obligations where the evidence is thin or the control is missing.
4. Check that the controls cover the full data lifecycle, including deletion.
5. Write the gaps with the risk and the remediation step for each.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
