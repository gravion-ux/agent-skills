# Review a marketing claim by testing the evidence behind every factual statement it makes

## Purpose

Review a marketing claim by testing the evidence behind every factual statement it makes.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to check that a marketing or advertising claim will stand up to scrutiny.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. List every factual claim in the copy and separate them from the opinion statements.
2. Check each factual claim against the evidence the business can actually produce.
3. Check the qualifiers, the disclaimers and the comparison basis for anything measured.
4. Check the categories of claims that are restricted, such as health, financial or environmental ones.
5. Flag each claim that needs substantiation and keep the copy versioned until it is cleared.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
