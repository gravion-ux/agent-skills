# Prepare for a regulatory inspection by assembling the evidence before the inspector arrives

## Purpose

Prepare for a regulatory inspection by assembling the evidence before the inspector arrives.

## When to use

Use when the user has been told an inspection, audit or visit is coming.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. Confirm the scope, the date and the documents the regulator will expect.
2. Gather the policies, the records and the evidence for each area in the scope.
3. Test the evidence by checking that it answers the question before it is asked.
4. Brief the people who will be interviewed on the scope and the escalation route.
5. Prepare a room, a document set and a written log of what is handed over.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
