# Write a policy that people can follow and route it through the approval it actually needs

## Purpose

Write a policy that people can follow and route it through the approval it actually needs.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to draft an internal policy and have it adopted.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. State the purpose and the scope, including who the policy does and does not cover.
2. Write the rules as actions people take, not as statements of intent.
3. Add the exceptions and the process for requesting one.
4. Name the owner, the review date and the version.
5. Send it to the approvers and the people who will be affected, and record the decision.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
