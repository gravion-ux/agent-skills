# Plan a record retention schedule that states what is kept, for how long and who disposes of it

## Purpose

Plan a record retention schedule that states what is kept, for how long and who disposes of it.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a retention policy for the records the business creates.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. List the record types with the legal or operational reason they are held.
2. Set the retention period for each type from the source obligation, not from habit.
3. State the trigger that starts the clock, such as the end of the tax year.
4. Define the disposal method and record the disposal in a log.
5. State the legal hold procedure so records are not destroyed while a matter is open.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
