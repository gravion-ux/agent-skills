# Prepare a data processing agreement that covers the processor's duties and the audit rights

## Purpose

Prepare a data processing agreement that covers the processor's duties and the audit rights.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a data processing agreement for a vendor that handles personal data.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. Describe the processing, the data types and the purposes in specific terms.
2. List the processor's obligations for security, staff, sub-processors and incident notification.
3. Set the time limits for returning or deleting the data when the contract ends.
4. State the audit rights and the assistance the processor must give with inspections.
5. Have a qualified reviewer check the jurisdiction and the regulator requirements before signing.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
