# Research a regulation by working from the official source and recording each obligation

## Purpose

Research a regulation by working from the official source and recording each obligation.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to know whether a regulation applies to them and what it requires.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. Start from the regulator's own guidance rather than a summary or a blog post.
2. Write the scope and test each part of it against the organisation's actual activity.
3. Record each obligation with the section reference that creates it.
4. Note the dates that start, change or end each obligation.
5. State the conclusion with the source links and flag any point that needs a legal opinion.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
