# Review a contract by extracting the terms that decide who wins when things go wrong

## Purpose

Review a contract by extracting the terms that decide who wins when things go wrong.

## When to use

Use when the user wants the important clauses of a contract identified before they sign it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. Read the whole document once for the structure before looking at any clause in detail.
2. Extract the scope, the term, the renewal and the termination rights.
3. Note the payment terms, the liability caps and the indemnities on both sides.
4. List the clauses that are unusual, silent or favourable only to one party.
5. Flag each point for a qualified reviewer with the risk written out in one line.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
