# Triage a legal question into what is known, what is urgent and what needs a qualified opinion

## Purpose

Triage a legal question into what is known, what is urgent and what needs a qualified opinion.

## When to use

Use when the user has a legal question and needs to know how to act before counsel is available.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. Write the question in one sentence with the facts that change the answer.
2. Identify the deadline, if any, and the deadline for responding to it.
3. Check the organisation's own contracts and policies for the governing terms.
4. List what is genuinely uncertain and what would change the answer.
5. Give the temporary steps that are safe to take and name what must wait for an opinion.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
