# Keep a register of every licence and notice so the obligations can be met when they are due

## Purpose

Keep a register of every licence and notice so the obligations can be met when they are due.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a maintained list of the licences and notices that apply to the organisation.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. List every component with its licence, its version and the notice file it ships.
2. Record each obligation the licence creates, such as attribution or source disclosure.
3. Note where the notice text must appear and where the copyright notice must be kept.
4. Link each entry to the place in the repository or product where the notice is published.
5. Review the register whenever a dependency is added or upgraded.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
