# Prepare a training attestation list that tracks who completed what and who still owes it

## Purpose

Prepare a training attestation list that tracks who completed what and who still owes it.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to run a compliance training and track the acknowledgements.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. List the training, the policy it covers and the deadline for completing it.
2. List every person who must complete it, including new starters and contractors.
3. Send the training with the deadline and a route to ask questions.
4. Track completion per person and follow up with the people who have not finished.
5. Keep the signed attestations with the training version they refer to.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
