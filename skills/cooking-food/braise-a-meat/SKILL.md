# Braise meat slowly in liquid until it is tender, with a clear browning and temperature plan

## Purpose

Braise meat slowly in liquid until it is tender, with a clear browning and temperature plan.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to cook a tough cut of meat slowly in a braising liquid.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Season and brown the meat on all sides in a hot pan with fat.
2. Remove the meat and cook the aromatics and the flour in the same pan.
3. Deglaze the pan and add enough liquid to come halfway up the meat.
4. Braise covered at a low, steady heat until a fork enters without resistance.
5. Rest the meat before carving and reduce the sauce separately for the final flavour.
