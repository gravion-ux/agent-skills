# Repair a broken sauce by finding whether the fat or the liquid separated and bringing it back

## Purpose

Repair a broken sauce by finding whether the fat or the liquid separated and bringing it back.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to rescue a sauce that split, curdled or turned grainy.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Identify whether the emulsion broke from heat, over-whisking or the wrong fat.
2. Strain out the curdled solids if the sauce is lumpy rather than simply oily.
3. Bring the sauce back to the right temperature before attempting to emulsify it again.
4. Add the fat slowly while whisking, starting with a small amount and building up.
5. Loosen the sauce with a little warm liquid if it is too thick to come together.

A scaled recipe with timings, temperatures, substitutions and the order of operations.
