# Scale a recipe up or down for a different number of people without breaking the ratios

## Purpose

Scale a recipe up or down for a different number of people without breaking the ratios.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to cook a recipe for a different number of servings.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Multiply every ingredient by the ratio of the new servings to the original.
2. Scale seasoning and spice by the same ratio, then adjust to taste at the end.
3. Increase the pan size and the cooking time when the volume grows substantially.
4. Keep the dough and batter resting times unchanged, because they scale with time rather than quantity.
5. Check that the pan holds the new volume with room for the food to move.

## Output contract

A scaled recipe with timings, temperatures, substitutions and the order of operations.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The quantities scale linearly with the servings
- [ ] Allergens are called out explicitly
- [ ] Food-safety timings are respected

## Safety notes

Never suggest a workaround for a serious allergy, and state when food must be cooked to a safe internal temperature.
