# Sharpen a kitchen knife on a whetstone and keep it cutting cleanly afterwards

## Purpose

Sharpen a kitchen knife on a whetstone and keep it cutting cleanly afterwards.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to sharpen a blunt kitchen knife properly.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Identify the edge angle from the maker's guidance, usually fifteen or twenty degrees per side.
2. Soak the whetstone and flatten it before the first pass.
3. Work the edge along the stone with even pressure on both sides of the blade.
4. Raise the angle slightly for a refining pass on each face.
5. Remove the burr, hone on a finer stone and test the edge on a piece of paper.
