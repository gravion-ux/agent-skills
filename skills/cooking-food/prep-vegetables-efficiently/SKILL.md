# Prep vegetables in one organised session with a clear order and minimal washing

## Purpose

Prep vegetables in one organised session with a clear order and minimal washing.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to batch-prep vegetables without wasting time or water.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Gather every vegetable before cutting so nothing is forgotten at the end.
2. Wash the whole batch once in a bowl of cold water rather than under a tap.
3. Cut in order of decreasing time to cook, starting with the densest vegetables.
4. Keep each vegetable in its own pile so it can go straight to the pan.
5. Move prepared vegetables into containers with a towel to absorb surface moisture.

## Output contract

A scaled recipe with timings, temperatures, substitutions and the order of operations.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The quantities scale linearly with the servings
- [ ] Allergens are called out explicitly
- [ ] Food-safety timings are respected

## Safety notes

Never suggest a workaround for a serious allergy, and state when food must be cooked to a safe internal temperature.
