# Batch-cook a week of meals that reheat well and still taste worth eating

## Purpose

Batch-cook a week of meals that reheat well and still taste worth eating.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to prepare several meals at once for the coming week.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Choose two or three dishes that share base ingredients to reduce the shopping list.
2. Cook the longest dish first so it finishes cooling before it is portioned.
3. Portion into containers the user can pick up with one hand.
4. Label each container with the dish and the date it was cooked.
5. Freeze the portions that will not be eaten within three days.

## Output contract

A scaled recipe with timings, temperatures, substitutions and the order of operations.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The quantities scale linearly with the servings
- [ ] Allergens are called out explicitly
- [ ] Food-safety timings are respected

## Safety notes

Never suggest a workaround for a serious allergy, and state when food must be cooked to a safe internal temperature.
