# Reduce food waste in a kitchen by planning purchases around what is already there

## Purpose

Reduce food waste in a kitchen by planning purchases around what is already there.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to cut the food waste in their household kitchen.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. List what is in the kitchen now, including the freezer and the dry store.
2. Plan meals around the ingredients that will spoil soonest.
3. Shop from a list built on that plan rather than browsing in the aisle.
4. Label and date leftovers so they are used before they turn.
5. Keep a bin of what gets thrown away each week and review it for patterns.

## Output contract

A scaled recipe with timings, temperatures, substitutions and the order of operations.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The quantities scale linearly with the servings
- [ ] Allergens are called out explicitly
- [ ] Food-safety timings are respected

## Safety notes

Never suggest a workaround for a serious allergy, and state when food must be cooked to a safe internal temperature.
