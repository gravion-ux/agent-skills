# Cook rice evenly with the right water ratio, a covered pot and a resting period

## Purpose

Cook rice evenly with the right water ratio, a covered pot and a resting period.

## When to use

Use when the user wants reliably fluffy rice rather than mushy or scorched rice.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear to remove surface starch.
2. Use the water ratio for the grain type and adjust for the rice being older than a year.
3. Bring the water to a boil before adding the rice, then cover and turn the heat low.
4. Cook without lifting the lid until the steam subsides and the liquid is absorbed.
5. Rest the covered pot off the heat for ten minutes before fluffing with a fork.
