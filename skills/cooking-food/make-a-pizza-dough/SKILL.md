# Make pizza dough that is mixed, rested and shaped so it stretches without tearing

## Purpose

Make pizza dough that is mixed, rested and shaped so it stretches without tearing.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to make pizza dough from scratch for a home oven.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Mix the flour, water, salt and a little oil until no dry patches remain.
2. Rest the dough for at least thirty minutes before stretching it.
3. Knead briefly after the rest, then let it rise until it has doubled in size.
4. Divide the dough into balls and rest them again so the gluten relaxes.
5. Stretch each ball on a floured surface, keeping the rim untouched until the very end.
