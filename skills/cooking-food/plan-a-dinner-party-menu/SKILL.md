# Plan a dinner party menu that flows from starter to dessert and fits the kitchen's capacity

## Purpose

Plan a dinner party menu that flows from starter to dessert and fits the kitchen's capacity.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to host a dinner and needs a menu that works as a whole.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Confirm the number of guests, the dietary restrictions and the time available.
2. Choose dishes that can be made ahead so only one thing is finished on the night.
3. Check that the oven, hob and fridge are not all needed at the same moment.
4. Sequence the courses so the cold dish is ready before the hot one is plated.
5. Plan the timing backwards from the serving time using each dish's rest and chill time.

## Output contract

A scaled recipe with timings, temperatures, substitutions and the order of operations.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The quantities scale linearly with the servings
- [ ] Allergens are called out explicitly
- [ ] Food-safety timings are respected

## Safety notes

Never suggest a workaround for a serious allergy, and state when food must be cooked to a safe internal temperature.
