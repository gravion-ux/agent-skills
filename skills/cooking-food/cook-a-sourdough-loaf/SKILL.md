# Bake a sourdough loaf with a fermentation that is judged by the dough rather than the clock

## Purpose

Bake a sourdough loaf with a fermentation that is judged by the dough rather than the clock.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to bake an open-crumb sourdough loaf at home.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for nutrition planning over weeks; use health-wellness instead.

## Inputs required

- The dish, the servings and the equipment
- The dietary constraints and the allergies
- The time available

## Procedure

1. Mix the flour and water and rest the dough before adding the starter and salt.
2. Let the dough rise at room temperature until it has visibly grown and shows bubbles.
3. Fold the dough several times during the rise to build structure.
4. Shape the loaf, place it in a floured basket and refrigerate it overnight.
5. Bake it in a covered pot so the crust stays soft while the crumb sets.
