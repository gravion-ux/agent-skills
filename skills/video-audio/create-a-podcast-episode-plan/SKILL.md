# Plan a podcast episode with a segment order, a time budget and a clear arc for the listener

## Purpose

Plan a podcast episode with a segment order, a time budget and a clear arc for the listener.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to outline an episode before recording it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Write the single question the episode answers for the listener.
2. Break the episode into segments and give each one a time budget.
3. Order the segments so the strongest material sits in the middle.
4. Note the supporting material each segment needs before recording.
5. Write the open and the close so the episode has a clear shape.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
