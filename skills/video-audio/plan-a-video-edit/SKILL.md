# Plan a video edit as an assembly list of cuts, inserts and audio changes before touching the timeline

## Purpose

Plan a video edit as an assembly list of cuts, inserts and audio changes before touching the timeline.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to plan how a source recording becomes a finished edit.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. List every source clip with its in and out points and what it contributes.
2. Order the clips so each one sets up the next.
3. Mark the cut points where a transition, a title card or a cutaway sits.
4. Note the audio treatment for each section, including music and level changes.
5. Check the planned runtime against the target before starting the assembly.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
