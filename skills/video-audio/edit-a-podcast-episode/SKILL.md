# Edit a podcast episode by tightening the conversation, removing dead air and levelling both voices

## Purpose

Edit a podcast episode by tightening the conversation, removing dead air and levelling both voices.

## When to use

Use when the user has a raw podcast recording that needs to become an episode.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Mark the sections that advance the conversation and cut the rest.
2. Remove the long pauses, the false starts and the crosstalk.
3. Level both voices to a consistent loudness and remove the mouth clicks.
4. Add the intro, the outro and the music stings at the agreed points.
5. Write the episode notes from the final cut, not from the raw recording.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
