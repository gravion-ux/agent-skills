# Design a background music bed that follows the pace of the edit without drawing attention to itself

## Purpose

Design a background music bed that follows the pace of the edit without drawing attention to itself.

## When to use

Use when the user wants music placed under a longer piece of video.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Pick the tempo that matches the edit's rhythm and stay in one key.
2. Write the arc with the sections where the energy rises and where it settles.
3. Choose the sections of the edit that need silence instead of music.
4. Cut the track to the edit and fade the ends to match the picture.
5. Confirm the bed never competes with the speech at full volume.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
