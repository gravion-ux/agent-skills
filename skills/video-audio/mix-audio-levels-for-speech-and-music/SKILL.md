# Mix speech and music so the voice stays in front and the music never masks a word

## Purpose

Mix speech and music so the voice stays in front and the music never masks a word.

## When to use

Use when the user wants dialogue and a music bed balanced in one mix.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Set the speech level first and treat it as the reference for the mix.
2. Set the music so it sits below the speech by a consistent margin.
3. Lower the music further under every spoken section and restore it in the gaps.
4. Check the mix on both headphones and a phone speaker.
5. Watch the peaks and bring the loudest moments back under the ceiling.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
