# Record a demo video with clean state, deliberate pacing and a retake plan for each section

## Purpose

Record a demo video with clean state, deliberate pacing and a retake plan for each section.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to capture a product demo for publishing.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Set the application to a known state with the sample data already loaded.
2. Turn off notifications and hide the personal parts of the screen.
3. Record each section separately so one mistake does not cost the whole take.
4. Pause briefly after each action so the viewer can register what changed.
5. Watch the full recording once and mark the sections that need a retake.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
