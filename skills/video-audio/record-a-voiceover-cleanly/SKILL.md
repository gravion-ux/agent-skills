# Record a voiceover with a treated room, a fixed microphone position and a quiet warm up

## Purpose

Record a voiceover with a treated room, a fixed microphone position and a quiet warm up.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to capture a clean voice track for a video or podcast.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Record the room tone for ten seconds before speaking.
2. Position the microphone at mouth height with a pop filter in place.
3. Keep the same distance from the microphone for every take.
4. Close the door, silence the phone and mute the desktop notifications.
5. Record a short test line and check it for hum, plosives and room echo.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
