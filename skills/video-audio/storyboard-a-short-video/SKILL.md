# Storyboard a short video as a sequence of frames that each carry one idea

## Purpose

Storyboard a short video as a sequence of frames that each carry one idea.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a visual plan for a short piece of video.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. List the beats the video must hit in order, one idea per beat.
2. Draw a rough frame for each beat with the framing and the subject marked.
3. Write the camera move and the transition under each frame.
4. Note the on-screen text and the narration line that belong to each frame.
5. Check the total frame count against the target duration.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
