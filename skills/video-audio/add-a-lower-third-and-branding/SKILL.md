# Add a lower third and branding that identify the speaker without covering the content

## Purpose

Add a lower third and branding that identify the speaker without covering the content.

## When to use

Use when the user wants titles, names and marks placed on a finished video.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Write the lower third text and keep it to a name and one role.
2. Set the safe area so the lower third clears the captions and the frame edge.
3. Place the mark in a corner where it stays visible through the whole edit.
4. Animate the lower third on and off over two frames.
5. Watch one section with the lower third and one without before exporting.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
