# Write a video description and metadata that tell a viewer what they get and where to go next

## Purpose

Write a video description and metadata that tell a viewer what they get and where to go next.

## When to use

Use when the user needs the publishing details that sit beside a finished video.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Derive the chapter list from the scene changes in the final cut.
2. Open with one sentence that states what the video shows and who it is for.
3. Write three phrases a searcher would actually type.
4. Name the tools, links and credits the video depends on.
5. Check the title length against the platform limit before publishing.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
