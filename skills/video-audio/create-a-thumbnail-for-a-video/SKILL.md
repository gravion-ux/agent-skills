# Create a thumbnail that reads at a small size and makes the subject of the video obvious

## Purpose

Create a thumbnail that reads at a small size and makes the subject of the video obvious.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a still image that represents a video in a list.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Pick the frame that shows the result rather than the setup.
2. Crop it to the aspect ratio the platform renders and keep the subject large.
3. Add a short title of at most five words with high contrast over the image.
4. Check the thumbnail at the size it appears in a feed.
5. Save the thumbnail with the video in the publishing folder.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
