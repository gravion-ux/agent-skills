# Write a shot list for a product film that names every frame, its size and the purpose it serves

## Purpose

Write a shot list for a product film that names every frame, its size and the purpose it serves.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a shot list for a filmed product piece.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. List the story beats the film has to carry in order.
2. Write one row per shot with the shot size, the subject and the movement.
3. Add the duration, the location and the audio note for each shot.
4. Mark the shots that can be reused as short vertical clips.
5. Number the shots and carry that numbering into the call sheet.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
