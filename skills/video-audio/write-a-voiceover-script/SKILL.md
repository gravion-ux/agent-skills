# Write a voiceover script that is written for the ear and fits the picture it sits over

## Purpose

Write a voiceover script that is written for the ear and fits the picture it sits over.

## When to use

Use when the user needs words for a voiceover track on existing footage.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a still image; use image-visual instead.

## Inputs required

- The source material and the target duration
- The format, the channels and the captions
- The rights and the music licensing constraints

## Procedure

1. Watch the footage and write the beats the narration must land on.
2. Write short sentences that can be spoken in one breath.
3. Mark the read, the pause and the emphasis in the script.
4. Leave the silence where the picture needs to be seen without words.
5. Read it aloud against the footage and adjust the length to the shot.

## Output contract

An edited audio or video file with a transcript or caption track and the source list.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The audio is intelligible at normal volume
- [ ] Captions or a transcript are attached
- [ ] Every clip is licensed

## Safety notes

Never publish audio or footage of a person without consent, and never strip attribution from licensed media.
