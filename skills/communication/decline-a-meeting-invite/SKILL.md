# Decline a meeting invite with a clear no and a reason the organiser can act on

## Purpose

Decline a meeting invite with a clear no and a reason the organiser can act on.

## When to use

Use when the user cannot attend a meeting and needs to decline it properly.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Reply promptly even if the decision takes a little time to make.
2. Say clearly that the user cannot attend rather than leaving it ambiguous.
3. Give a real reason in one line, without over-explaining or apologising repeatedly.
4. Offer an alternative, such as a written update or a shorter call.
5. Decline the calendar invite itself so the slot is released.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
