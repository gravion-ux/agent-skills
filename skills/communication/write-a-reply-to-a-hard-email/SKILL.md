# Reply to a difficult email by staying factual, addressing every point and not escalating

## Purpose

Reply to a difficult email by staying factual, addressing every point and not escalating.

## When to use

Use when the user has received an email that is blunt, accusatory or hostile.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Read it twice and list every point and question that was raised.
2. Write the factual answer first, without reacting to the tone.
3. Answer each point in the order it was raised, so nothing looks avoided.
4. Correct any factual error calmly, with the evidence.
5. End with the next step and leave the tone out of the reply.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
