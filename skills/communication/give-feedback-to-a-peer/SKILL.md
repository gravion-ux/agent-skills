# Give a peer feedback that is specific, kind and about the work rather than the person

## Purpose

Give a peer feedback that is specific, kind and about the work rather than the person.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to give honest feedback to someone at the same level.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Choose a moment when neither of you is rushed or interrupted.
2. Describe the specific behaviour and its effect on the work.
3. Make the observation before the interpretation.
4. Be clear about what you would like to see differently, and what would be better.
5. Ask for their view of it, because they may know something you do not.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
