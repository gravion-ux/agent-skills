# Deliver bad news by stating it clearly and attaching the plan that goes with it

## Purpose

Deliver bad news by stating it clearly and attaching the plan that goes with it.

## When to use

Use when the user has to tell people that something has not gone as planned.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State the news in the first two lines, without softening it into ambiguity.
2. Give the facts behind it briefly and only what the audience needs.
3. Attach the plan for what happens next, with owners and dates.
4. Say what you need from the audience, if anything.
5. Offer to talk it through, and give a time to do so.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
