# Write a follow-up that recaps the decisions and lists the actions with their owners

## Purpose

Write a follow-up that recaps the decisions and lists the actions with their owners.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to confirm what was agreed after a meeting or a call.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. List the decisions that were made, one line each.
2. List each action with its owner and the date it is due.
3. Include the questions that are still open and who will answer them.
4. Add the links or documents that were referred to.
5. State the date of the next check-in if one was agreed.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
