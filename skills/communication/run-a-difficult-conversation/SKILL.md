# Run a difficult conversation by preparing the facts, the outcome and the room

## Purpose

Run a difficult conversation by preparing the facts, the outcome and the room.

## When to use

Use when the user has to hold a conversation that is likely to be uncomfortable.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Write the facts you can state without interpretation or exaggeration.
2. Decide what outcome you want from the conversation and what you would accept.
3. Check the setting, the timing and who else needs to be present.
4. Open with the facts and the purpose rather than with the feelings.
5. Agree the next steps and the date for checking them.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
