# Write a status update that reports progress, blockers and the next step without padding

## Purpose

Write a status update that reports progress, blockers and the next step without padding.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to report where a piece of work has got to.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State the current position in one sentence, against the expected position.
2. List what is done and what is in progress, with no more than three items each.
3. Name the blocker and who is needed to clear it.
4. Give the next milestone and the date it is expected.
5. Keep it short enough to be read in under thirty seconds.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
