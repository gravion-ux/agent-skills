# Communicate in a crisis hour by stating the facts, the impact and the next update time

## Purpose

Communicate in a crisis hour by stating the facts, the impact and the next update time.

## When to use

Use when the user is dealing with an incident and needs to tell people what is happening.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State what is known, what is not known and what is being investigated.
2. Say what the impact is for each affected group.
3. Name the owner of the response and what they are doing right now.
4. Give the time of the next update, and keep to it.
5. Do not speculate on the cause or on the resolution time.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
