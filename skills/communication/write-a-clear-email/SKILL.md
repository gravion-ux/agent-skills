# Write an email whose ask, context and next step are clear in the first three lines

## Purpose

Write an email whose ask, context and next step are clear in the first three lines.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to send an email that will not need a follow-up question.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Write the subject line so it states the topic and the action needed.
2. Open with the ask or the outcome, before any background.
3. Add only the context the reader needs to act, and move the rest to the end.
4. State the next step with its owner and the date.
5. Read it once as the recipient and remove anything that does not serve the ask.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
