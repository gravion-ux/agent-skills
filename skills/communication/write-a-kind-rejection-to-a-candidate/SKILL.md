# Reject a candidate kindly with a clear decision, respect and something concrete to take away

## Purpose

Reject a candidate kindly with a clear decision, respect and something concrete to take away.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to tell an unsuccessful candidate that they were not selected.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State the decision clearly and early, so the candidate is not left guessing.
2. Thank them for the time and the effort they put in.
3. Give one specific, honest reason the role did not match at this time.
4. Avoid any statement that could be read as a judgement of their worth.
5. Close with what happens with their application and offer to keep in touch.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
