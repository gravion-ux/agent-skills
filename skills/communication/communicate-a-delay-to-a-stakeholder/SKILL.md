# Communicate a delay with the new date, the reason and what is being done about it

## Purpose

Communicate a delay with the new date, the reason and what is being done about it.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to tell a stakeholder that something will be late.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State the new expected date in the first line.
2. Give the reason in one sentence, focusing on the cause rather than the story.
3. Describe what was done to recover the schedule and what is being done now.
4. State what is unaffected, because that is the part the reader most needs.
5. Ask for a decision only if you genuinely need one, and say what the options are.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
