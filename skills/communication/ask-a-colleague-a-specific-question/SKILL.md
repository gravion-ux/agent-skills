# Ask a colleague a question that can be answered directly instead of prompting a negotiation

## Purpose

Ask a colleague a question that can be answered directly instead of prompting a negotiation.

## When to use

Use when the user needs information from someone else and the ask is not yet clear.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State what you are trying to do, in one or two sentences.
2. Ask the one question that would unblock you.
3. Say why you are asking it, so the answer can be pitched correctly.
4. Offer a deadline and say what you will do if there is no answer.
5. Make it easy to reply with a short answer rather than a whole document.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
