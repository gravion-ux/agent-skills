# Present a result to an executive by leading with the decision and the number behind it

## Purpose

Present a result to an executive by leading with the decision and the number behind it.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to present the outcome of work to a senior audience.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State the result and the decision needed in the first thirty seconds.
2. Give the one number that shows whether the result is good.
3. Explain what it means for the business, in plain terms.
4. Name the risk and the cost of doing nothing.
5. End with the specific decision and who makes it by when.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
