# Ask for feedback on your own work by naming what you want judged and when you need it

## Purpose

Ask for feedback on your own work by naming what you want judged and when you need it.

## When to use

Use when the user wants useful feedback on something they have produced.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. State what you produced and what decision it feeds.
2. Name the specific questions you want answered, not just ask for general feedback.
3. Suggest who would give the most useful view, based on what they have seen.
4. Give a deadline that respects the other person's time.
5. Ask for one thing to keep and one thing to change, rather than a list.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
