# Give a toast at a team event that names the people and the moments that mattered

## Purpose

Give a toast at a team event that names the people and the moments that mattered.

## When to use

Use when the user has to say a few words at a celebration for a team.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Plan two or three moments you want to mention, rather than a list of names.
2. Name the people who did the work, specifically and by name.
3. Tell one short story that shows the team in a real moment.
4. Include one person who is not in the room but who made the work possible.
5. Finish with a short, warm line that does not overstay its welcome.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
