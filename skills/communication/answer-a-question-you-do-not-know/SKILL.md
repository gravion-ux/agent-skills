# Answer a question you do not know by saying so clearly and offering a route to the answer

## Purpose

Answer a question you do not know by saying so clearly and offering a route to the answer.

## When to use

Use when the user is asked something they cannot answer accurately.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Say plainly that the answer is not known rather than guessing.
2. State what is known so far, if anything, and how reliable it is.
3. Say who would know and how to reach them.
4. Offer to find the answer and say when it will be available.
5. Avoid filling the gap with a confident-sounding approximation.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
