# Reply to a customer complaint by acknowledging it, explaining it and committing to a next step

## Purpose

Reply to a customer complaint by acknowledging it, explaining it and committing to a next step.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to respond to an unhappy customer.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Acknowledge the specific complaint in the customer's own terms.
2. Explain what happened without blame and without hiding behind process language.
3. State what you are doing now and by when.
4. Offer the remedy that fits the impact they described.
5. Offer a direct contact route for anything that is still unresolved.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
