# Write a meeting invite whose agenda says what will be decided and who prepares what

## Purpose

Write a meeting invite whose agenda says what will be decided and who prepares what.

## When to use

Use when the user is organising a meeting and wants people to arrive prepared.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Name the decision or outcome the meeting is for, in the title.
2. List each agenda item with the time it will take.
3. Assign a lead for each item and say who needs to prepare.
4. Mark the items that are for information only.
5. State the pre-reading and the deadline for reading it.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
