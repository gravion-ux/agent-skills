# Summarise a long email thread into the decisions, the open questions and the next actions

## Purpose

Summarise a long email thread into the decisions, the open questions and the next actions.

## When to use

Use when the user has a long thread and needs to catch up or hand it on.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Read the thread from the oldest message to the newest to see how the position changed.
2. List the decisions that were actually made, not the ones that were discussed.
3. List the questions still open with the person who owes the answer.
4. Record the actions and their owners with the dates.
5. State the current position in two sentences at the top.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
