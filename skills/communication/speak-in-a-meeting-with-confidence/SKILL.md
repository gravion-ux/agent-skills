# Speak in a meeting by preparing the point, the evidence and the ask before it starts

## Purpose

Speak in a meeting by preparing the point, the evidence and the ask before it starts.

## When to use

Use when the user is nervous about presenting or speaking in a meeting.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Write the single point you want the room to remember.
2. Prepare one piece of evidence and one number that support it.
3. Write the ask, so the meeting ends with a decision rather than a discussion.
4. Rehearse the first thirty seconds aloud, because the opening sets the tone.
5. Plan how to take notes during the meeting so you are not searching for words.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
