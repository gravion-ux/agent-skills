# Reschedule a meeting by offering two concrete times and keeping the reason to one line

## Purpose

Reschedule a meeting by offering two concrete times and keeping the reason to one line.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to move a meeting without over-apologising for it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for formal published documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and their prior knowledge
- The message and the outcome wanted
- The channel and the deadline

## Procedure

1. Acknowledge the existing booking in one line.
2. Give the specific reason the slot no longer works.
3. Offer two alternative times that work for the user.
4. Ask for the earliest reply so the slot can be released if there is none.
5. Reconfirm once a new time is agreed.

## Output contract

A message ready to send, with the ask, the context and the next step made explicit.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The ask is stated in one sentence
- [ ] The tone matches the audience and channel
- [ ] The next step and its owner are clear

## Safety notes

Never send a message on the user's behalf without explicit approval of the final text.
