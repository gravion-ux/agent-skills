# Review a supplement or diet claim against its evidence, dose and safety before acting

## Purpose

Review a supplement or diet claim against its evidence, dose and safety before acting.

## When to use

Use when the user has seen a health claim and wants it checked before they try it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Write the claim in one sentence, including the promised effect and the population.
2. Find what kind of study supports it and how large and how long it was.
3. Check the studied dose against the marketed dose and the proposed duration.
4. Look for interactions and contraindications with the user's medication and conditions.
5. State plainly what is well supported, what is uncertain and what to ask a clinician.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
