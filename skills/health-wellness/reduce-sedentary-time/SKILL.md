# Reduce sedentary time by replacing long stretches of sitting with small, frequent movements

## Purpose

Reduce sedentary time by replacing long stretches of sitting with small, frequent movements.

## When to use

Use when the user sits for long periods and wants to move more during the day.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Find the longest unbroken block of sitting in the user's normal day.
2. Set a timer for that block and add one movement break at the end of it.
3. Choose movements that need no equipment and can be done standing.
4. Link each break to an existing cue, such as a meeting ending.
5. Add standing or walking to the routines that are already daily, such as phone calls.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
