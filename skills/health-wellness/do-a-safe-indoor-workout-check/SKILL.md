# Check an indoor workout space for hazards before exercising in it

## Purpose

Check an indoor workout space for hazards before exercising in it.

## When to use

Use when the user is about to exercise indoors and wants to make sure the space is safe.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Check the floor is clear, dry and free of anything that catches a foot.
2. Confirm the space has enough clearance for the movements and for other people.
3. Check that any equipment is stable and in good condition before relying on it.
4. Make sure the room is ventilated and not dangerously hot.
5. Know where the nearest exit is and keep a phone or watch within reach.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
