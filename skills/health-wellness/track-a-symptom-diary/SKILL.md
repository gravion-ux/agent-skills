# Track symptoms on a simple diary so patterns become visible before an appointment

## Purpose

Track symptoms on a simple diary so patterns become visible before an appointment.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to record symptoms to find a pattern over time.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Choose the symptoms to record and keep the list short enough to keep up.
2. Record the date, time, severity and any note about what happened that day.
3. Add a column for the likely triggers, such as sleep, food or activity.
4. Record a daily rating of overall severity as well.
5. Review the diary before an appointment and bring the pattern with it.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
