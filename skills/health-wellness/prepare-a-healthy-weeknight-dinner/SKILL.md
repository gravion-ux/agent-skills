# Prepare a healthy weeknight dinner in the time available without complicated techniques

## Purpose

Prepare a healthy weeknight dinner in the time available without complicated techniques.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a healthy dinner that is quick on an ordinary evening.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Fix the time limit and choose one technique that needs no attention at the end.
2. Build the plate around a protein, a vegetable and a whole-grain base.
3. Cut the preparation so that the long-cooked item starts first.
4. Use one flavour shortcut, such as a prepared sauce, to save time without adding a recipe.
5. Plate it while it is hot so the evening is not spent cleaning a second pan.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
