# Plan a home workout around the space, the equipment and the time actually available

## Purpose

Plan a home workout around the space, the equipment and the time actually available.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a workout routine they can do at home.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Measure the space and list the equipment that is genuinely available.
2. Choose movements that fit the space and do not need a partner.
3. Set the session length so it fits the real time slot, not the ideal one.
4. Order the movements from hardest to easiest so the hardest is done first.
5. Write the warm-up and the cool-down into the plan rather than leaving them out.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
