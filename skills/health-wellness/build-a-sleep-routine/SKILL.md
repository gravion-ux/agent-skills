# Build a sleep routine anchored on a consistent wake time and a wind-down sequence

## Purpose

Build a sleep routine anchored on a consistent wake time and a wind-down sequence.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to improve their sleep by changing their evening and morning routine.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Pick a fixed wake time that can be kept on weekends too.
2. Work backwards to set a consistent lights-out and wind-down time.
3. Define a short, repeatable wind-down sequence with no screens in the last part of it.
4. Remove the caffeine and heavy meal constraints from the previous day.
5. Track sleep for two weeks before changing anything else.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
