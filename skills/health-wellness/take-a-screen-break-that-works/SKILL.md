# Take a screen break that actually restores attention rather than scrolling through another feed

## Purpose

Take a screen break that actually restores attention rather than scrolling through another feed.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a break from screen work that reduces fatigue instead of adding to it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. Look away from every screen for at least twenty seconds before doing anything else.
2. Focus on something at a distance, such as a window, to relax the focusing muscles.
3. Stand up and move the shoulders and neck through their full range.
4. Do not scroll a feed during the break, because it keeps the attention system engaged.
5. Return to the work in a stated order, starting with the named next action.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
