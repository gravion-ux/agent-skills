# Eat well on a tight budget by planning around cheap staples and batch cooking

## Purpose

Eat well on a tight budget by planning around cheap staples and batch cooking.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to eat nutritious food without spending much money.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a medical diagnosis or an acute emergency; use a qualified clinician instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal and the current baseline
- The time, equipment and mobility available
- Any constraints the user has stated

## Procedure

1. List the cheap staples that are always available, such as grains, beans and eggs.
2. Plan two batch-cooked dishes that use the same base ingredients.
3. Buy the vegetables that are in season and cheap that week.
4. Keep one shelf-stable option for days when there is no time to cook.
5. Cook a larger batch than needed and freeze the portions immediately.

## Output contract

A realistic plan with weekly structure, a simple tracking method and clear stopping conditions.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan matches the stated baseline and constraints
- [ ] Progression is gradual and reversible
- [ ] The plan says when to seek professional advice

## Safety notes

Never diagnose, prescribe or set medical targets, and never encourage a routine that contradicts a clinician's advice.
