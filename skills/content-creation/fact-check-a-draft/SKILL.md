# Check every claim in a draft against a source and flag the ones that fail

## Purpose

Check every claim in a draft against a source and flag the ones that fail.

## When to use

Use when the user wants the factual claims in a draft verified.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. List each checkable claim with its date, figure and scope.
2. Locate a primary source for every claim and record its URL and date.
3. Mark each claim as confirmed, outdated, unsupported or disputed.
4. Rewrite or remove any claim that no source supports.
5. Return a claim-by-claim table with sources and unresolved items flagged.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
