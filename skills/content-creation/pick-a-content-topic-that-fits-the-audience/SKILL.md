# Choose one content topic that matches the audience's interests, gaps and level of expertise

## Purpose

Choose one content topic that matches the audience's interests, gaps and level of expertise.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to decide what to publish next for a defined audience.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. List the audience's recurring questions, goals and current knowledge gaps.
2. Score each candidate topic against audience interest, search demand and expertise.
3. Drop any topic the audience has no reason to care about yet.
4. Write one sentence naming the reader and the payoff the topic delivers.
5. Select the topic whose payoff can be delivered in a single piece.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
