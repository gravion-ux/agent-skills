# Write a short social post that opens with a hook and ends with one clear call to action

## Purpose

Write a short social post that opens with a hook and ends with one clear call to action.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a concise post for a social feed.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Set the single point the post must land in one reading pass.
2. Open with a concrete line that earns the next sentence.
3. Keep the body within the platform's character limit.
4. Close with one call to action naming the next step.
5. Deliver the draft marked as pending approval rather than published.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
