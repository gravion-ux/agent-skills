# Draft a complete long-form article with a title, hook, body and close

## Purpose

Draft a complete long-form article with a title, hook, body and close.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a finished article draft ready for editing.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Open with the hook that names the reader's problem in one sentence.
2. Deliver each outlined section in order with a concrete example or source.
3. Support every factual claim with a citation or attribution.
4. Keep paragraphs focused on one idea and cut repeated restatements.
5. Close with the takeaway and the single next action for the reader.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
