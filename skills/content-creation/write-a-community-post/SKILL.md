# Write a community post that answers a question and invites specific replies

## Purpose

Write a community post that answers a question and invites specific replies.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to post in a forum, group or community space.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Read the thread's rules and the original question before drafting.
2. Answer the question directly in the first two sentences.
3. Add one relevant detail from personal experience or a source.
4. Ask one specific follow-up question that extends the discussion.
5. Keep the tone plain and mark the draft for approval before posting.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
