# Write a case study that shows the problem, the chosen approach and the measured result

## Purpose

Write a case study that shows the problem, the chosen approach and the measured result.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a blog case study about a real customer result.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Collect the customer's starting problem, constraints and goal.
2. Quote the customer describing the result in their own words.
3. Describe the approach in sequence, naming what was changed and why.
4. Present the outcome with a figure the customer has approved and attributed.
5. Close with the takeaway and leave the case study unpublished for approval.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
