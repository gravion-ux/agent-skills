# Review published content against traffic, engagement and conversion goals

## Purpose

Review published content against traffic, engagement and conversion goals.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to assess how published content performed.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Set the goal each piece was meant to achieve before opening the metrics.
2. Compare traffic, read-through, engagement and conversion against that goal.
3. Identify the strongest and weakest sections by where readers stopped or clicked.
4. Name the one change most likely to move the metric for each weak piece.
5. Return a prioritised action list with the metric that supports each change.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
