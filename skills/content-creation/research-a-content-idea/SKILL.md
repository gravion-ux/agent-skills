# Gather sourced evidence for a content idea and turn it into an annotated research brief

## Purpose

Gather sourced evidence for a content idea and turn it into an annotated research brief.

## When to use

Use when the user needs reliable facts and examples to support a piece about a specific idea.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Write the central question the draft must answer.
2. Collect primary sources, interviews and data that address that question.
3. Record each source URL, publication date and the claim it supports.
4. Extract quotations and numbers with their original context intact.
5. Flag any claim that only one uncorroborated source supports.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
