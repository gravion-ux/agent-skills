# Write a one-page brief that gives a writer the angle, sources and constraints

## Purpose

Write a one-page brief that gives a writer the angle, sources and constraints.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to hand a writing assignment to another writer.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. State the target reader, the desired outcome and the publish date.
2. Define the angle in one sentence and list the points the piece must cover.
3. Attach the researched sources with URLs and the claims each one supports.
4. Specify the format, length, tone and the required title, hook and close.
5. Add the editor's name and the deadline for questions and the first draft.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
