# Triage a content backlog into priorities with owners and next actions

## Purpose

Triage a content backlog into priorities with owners and next actions.

## When to use

Use when the user has an unorganised list of content ideas to work through.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Collect every idea into one backlog with a source note for each.
2. Score each idea on audience demand, business fit and effort.
3. Move the top three items into next-up with an owner and a start date.
4. Label the rest as later, seasonal or dropped with a reason.
5. Keep a running list of what was shipped so ideas are not repeated.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
