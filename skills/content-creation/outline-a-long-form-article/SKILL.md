# Build a section-by-section outline for a long-form article with a clear through-line

## Purpose

Build a section-by-section outline for a long-form article with a clear through-line.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a long article structured before drafting begins.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. State the single promise the article makes to the reader.
2. Break the promise into sections that each advance one argument.
3. Assign each section a job: evidence, explanation, example or transition.
4. Open with the hook and close with the takeaway the reader can act on.
5. Add estimated word counts so no section dwarfs the rest.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
