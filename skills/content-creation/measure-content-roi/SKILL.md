# Measure content return by tying production cost to traffic, leads and revenue

## Purpose

Measure content return by tying production cost to traffic, leads and revenue.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to judge whether content spending paid off.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. List the direct cost of producing each piece, including allocated time.
2. Track the leads, signups and revenue each piece produced after publication.
3. Calculate cost per lead and revenue per piece using attributed figures only.
4. Compare the results across pieces by type and channel.
5. Flag any figure that depends on modelled or unverified data and label it an estimate.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
