# Turn one source piece into five platform-specific drafts without duplicating wording

## Purpose

Turn one source piece into five platform-specific drafts without duplicating wording.

## When to use

Use when the user wants one idea adapted into several channels.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Extract the single core claim from the source piece.
2. Map that claim to a blog post, short post, thread, video script and email.
3. Rewrite the opening line for each platform's format and length.
4. Adjust the call to action to match each channel's audience behaviour.
5. Deliver the five drafts together in a labelled list for review.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
