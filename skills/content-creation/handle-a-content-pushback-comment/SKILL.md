# Reply to a critical comment accurately, calmly and with a clear resolution

## Purpose

Reply to a critical comment accurately, calmly and with a clear resolution.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a reply to a critical comment on their content.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Read the full comment and identify the claim being challenged.
2. Check the challenged claim against the source before responding.
3. Draft a reply that restates the question and answers it with evidence.
4. Acknowledge a valid error plainly and state what will be corrected.
5. Deliver the reply for approval without posting it to the comment thread.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
