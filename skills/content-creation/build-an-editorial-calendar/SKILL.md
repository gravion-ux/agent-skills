# Map planned posts onto a dated editorial calendar with owners and channels

## Purpose

Map planned posts onto a dated editorial calendar with owners and channels.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a publishing schedule for upcoming content.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Collect the campaigns, launches and evergreen topics that need slots.
2. Assign each piece a publish date, channel, format and owner.
3. Space high-effort posts apart and avoid clashes between launches.
4. Place a review deadline two working days before each publish date.
5. Show the schedule in a dated table with gaps marked as unscheduled slots.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
