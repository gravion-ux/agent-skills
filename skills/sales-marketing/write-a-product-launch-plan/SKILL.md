# Write a product launch plan that ties the announcement to the assets and the timing

## Purpose

Write a product launch plan that ties the announcement to the assets and the timing.

## When to use

Use when the user is preparing to launch a product or a major feature.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Write the one-sentence positioning and who it is for.
2. List the assets needed, such as the page, the demo, the announcement and the support material.
3. Give each asset an owner and a deadline that falls before the launch date.
4. Plan the internal readiness, including the training, the documentation and the support scripts.
5. Set the metrics that will be checked in the first week and the first month.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
