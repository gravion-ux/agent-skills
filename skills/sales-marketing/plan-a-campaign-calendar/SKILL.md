# Plan a campaign calendar that sequences the messages around what the audience is doing

## Purpose

Plan a campaign calendar that sequences the messages around what the audience is doing.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a schedule for a campaign across several channels.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Fix the campaign dates, the budget and the single outcome the campaign serves.
2. List the moments in the audience's cycle that create attention for the message.
3. Place the launches, the reminders and the offers so they do not compete.
4. Name the owner and the channel for each item.
5. Leave space in the calendar for the reactive content that always comes up.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
