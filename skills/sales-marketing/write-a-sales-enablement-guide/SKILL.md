# Write a sales enablement guide that a new salesperson can use on their first call

## Purpose

Write a sales enablement guide that a new salesperson can use on their first call.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to prepare the material the sales team actually works from.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Start with what the product does and who it is for, in one page.
2. Write the discovery questions and the order they work best in.
3. Give the answers to the five objections that come up most.
4. Include the proof points and where the full case studies live.
5. Write what to do in the first week, so the guide is not only reference material.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
