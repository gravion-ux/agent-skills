# Run a discovery call by asking about the situation before proposing anything

## Purpose

Run a discovery call by asking about the situation before proposing anything.

## When to use

Use when the user has a call with a prospect to understand their problem.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Open with the context on the call and the outcome you hope for.
2. Ask about their current process and what it costs them today.
3. Find out what they have already tried and why it did not work.
4. Ask what a good outcome would look like and how they would measure it.
5. Close by summarising what you heard and agreeing the next step.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
