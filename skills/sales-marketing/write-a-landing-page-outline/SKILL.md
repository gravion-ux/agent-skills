# Write a landing page outline that answers the objections in the order they arise

## Purpose

Write a landing page outline that answers the objections in the order they arise.

## When to use

Use when the user wants the structure of a landing page before writing the copy.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Write the headline promise in the visitor's terms.
2. Add the subheadline that explains who it is for and what they get.
3. Place the proof, such as the evidence and the logos, right after the promise.
4. List the benefits as outcomes with the objection each one answers.
5. End with one call to action, repeated once, and nothing competing with it.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
