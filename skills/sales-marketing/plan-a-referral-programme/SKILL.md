# Plan a referral programme that gives both sides a reason and a simple way to act

## Purpose

Plan a referral programme that gives both sides a reason and a simple way to act.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to set up a customer referral scheme.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Define who can refer and who can be referred, and check the terms do not break the pricing model.
2. Choose the reward for the referrer and for the new customer separately.
3. Make the reward worth acting on without being larger than the first purchase margin.
4. Write the ask, the moment it is made and how simple it is to send a referral.
5. Measure referred customers separately, including how long they stay.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
