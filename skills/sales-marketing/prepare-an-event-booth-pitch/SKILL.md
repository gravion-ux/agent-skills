# Prepare an event pitch that opens with the visitor's problem rather than the product

## Purpose

Prepare an event pitch that opens with the visitor's problem rather than the product.

## When to use

Use when the user is staffing a booth or speaking at an event.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Write the question a visitor would ask that leads to the product.
2. Prepare a fifteen second answer and a sixty second version of it.
3. Prepare one demonstration that shows the product solving that problem.
4. Write the two questions to ask the visitor to find out whether it fits.
5. Plan the handout or the follow-up that the visitor leaves with.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
