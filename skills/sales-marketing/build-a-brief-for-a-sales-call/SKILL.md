# Build a brief for a sales call so the team walks in knowing the account and the goal

## Purpose

Build a brief for a sales call so the team walks in knowing the account and the goal.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a shared brief before a call with a prospect.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Summarise the account in three lines, including what they do and who we speak to.
2. State what we already know about their situation and where the gaps are.
3. Write the single outcome this call must achieve.
4. List the questions to ask and the objections likely to come up.
5. Assign who leads, who takes notes and who follows up afterwards.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
