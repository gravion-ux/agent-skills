# Segment a customer list by behaviour and need so each group gets a relevant message

## Purpose

Segment a customer list by behaviour and need so each group gets a relevant message.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to divide a customer list into groups for different campaigns.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. List the attributes you hold that describe what a customer actually uses.
2. Group the customers by the problem they have, not by demographics alone.
3. Check the size of each group and merge the ones too small to act on.
4. Write the message and the offer for each group separately.
5. Track the response by group so the segmentation is measured rather than assumed.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
