# Write a proposal that mirrors the client's problem, the approach and the outcome

## Purpose

Write a proposal that mirrors the client's problem, the approach and the outcome.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to send a written proposal to a client.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Restate the client's problem in their own words as the opening section.
2. Describe the approach as three phases with the outcome of each.
3. State clearly what is included, what is not included and what the client must provide.
4. Give the price, the payment schedule and the assumptions it depends on.
5. Add the terms, the start date and the acceptance step.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
