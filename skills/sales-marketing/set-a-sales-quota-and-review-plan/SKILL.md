# Set a sales quota with the review points that let it be corrected during the period

## Purpose

Set a sales quota with the review points that let it be corrected during the period.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to set a quota and the plan for reviewing it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Base the quota on the historical bookings for comparable roles and territories.
2. Check that the quota is achievable with the current headcount and the ramp time.
3. Set the review points at a quarter and two thirds of the period.
4. Define what happens at each review, such as a plan change or a reallocation of accounts.
5. Record the assumptions so the quota can be revised if they turn out to be wrong.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
