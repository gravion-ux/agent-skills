# Plan a webinar with a topic, a format and a follow-up that converts attendance

## Purpose

Plan a webinar with a topic, a format and a follow-up that converts attendance.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to run a webinar and needs the plan for it.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Choose a topic narrow enough that the right people would register for it.
2. Decide the format, such as a talk with questions or a demonstration with a panel.
3. Write the run of show with the timings for each segment.
4. Plan the promotion, the registration page and the reminder sequence.
5. Plan what happens after, including the follow-up to the registrants who did not attend.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
