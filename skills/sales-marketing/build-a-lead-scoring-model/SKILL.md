# Build a lead scoring model from the actions that have actually preceded a purchase

## Purpose

Build a lead scoring model from the actions that have actually preceded a purchase.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to prioritise leads by likelihood to buy.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Compare the features and actions of won accounts with those of lost ones.
2. Score each signal by how differently it appears between the two groups.
3. Give negative scores to the signals that appear in lost deals and not in won ones.
4. Set the thresholds for marketing qualified, sales ready and customer ready.
5. Review the model against a held-back month before relying on it.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
