# Manage a pipeline review by checking each deal against evidence rather than optimism

## Purpose

Manage a pipeline review by checking each deal against evidence rather than optimism.

## When to use

Use when the user runs a regular review of the sales pipeline with the team.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. List every open deal with its value, its stage and the date it entered that stage.
2. For each deal, name the specific evidence that it will close this period.
3. Remove the deals with no next meeting and no recent activity.
4. Check the value of the remaining deals against the target for the period.
5. Assign the next action and its date to every deal that stays in the pipeline.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
