# Qualify an inbound lead by checking fit, need, authority and timing before investing time

## Purpose

Qualify an inbound lead by checking fit, need, authority and timing before investing time.

## When to use

Use when the user has a new inbound enquiry and needs to decide if it is worth pursuing.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Identify who the person is and what organisation they are with.
2. Establish the problem they are trying to solve, in their own words.
3. Check the fit against the profile of a customer the business already serves well.
4. Ask who else is involved in the decision and what the budget situation is.
5. Agree the next step and the date, or mark the lead as not qualified and say why.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
