# Optimise a landing page by finding the one point where visitors leave

## Purpose

Optimise a landing page by finding the one point where visitors leave.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to improve the conversion rate of an existing landing page.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Check the traffic, the source and the device mix, because they change what to fix.
2. Look at where visitors drop off and what they click before they leave.
3. Check the promise against the search term or the ad that brought them in.
4. Test one change to the headline, the proof or the call to action at a time.
5. Keep the change only if the conversion improves and the sample size supports it.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
