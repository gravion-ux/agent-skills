# Review a lost deal to find the real reason rather than the one given on the call

## Purpose

Review a lost deal to find the real reason rather than the one given on the call.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to learn from a deal that was lost.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Record what the prospect actually said, in their own words.
2. Check the notes and the timeline against the stage the deal died at.
3. Look at the evidence they weighed, such as the price, the timing or the trust.
4. Compare the loss with the deals that were won to see the pattern.
5. Write one change to make and one thing to keep, with an owner for the change.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
