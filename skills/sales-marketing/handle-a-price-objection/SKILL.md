# Handle a price objection by returning the conversation to the value and the alternatives

## Purpose

Handle a price objection by returning the conversation to the value and the alternatives.

## When to use

Use when the user hears that a prospect considers the price too high.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Ask what the price is being compared against before responding.
2. Ask what budget they had in mind and what the cost of the problem is today.
3. Show the cost of the status quo using their own numbers where possible.
4. Offer a scoped option, such as a smaller first step, rather than only a discount.
5. Agree the next step, and do not apply a discount without the approval to do so.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
