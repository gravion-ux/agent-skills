# Write a cold outreach email that is short, specific and easy to reply to

## Purpose

Write a cold outreach email that is short, specific and easy to reply to.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to contact someone they have no existing relationship with.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Write one sentence showing why you contacted this person rather than a generic audience.
2. State the problem you can help with, in terms of their situation.
3. Give one piece of evidence that the claim is real, without overclaiming it.
4. Make one small, low-effort ask.
5. Keep it short enough to read in under thirty seconds and sign off simply.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
