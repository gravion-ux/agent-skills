# Follow up with a warm lead by adding something new rather than repeating the last message

## Purpose

Follow up with a warm lead by adding something new rather than repeating the last message.

## When to use

Use when the user has had contact with a prospect and needs to follow up without becoming a nuisance.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a product implementation plan; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The product and its differentiator
- The audience and the buying stage
- The proof points and the constraints on claims

## Procedure

1. Check what was already sent and what the response was.
2. Add one piece of information the person did not have, rather than restating the offer.
3. Reference the specific point from the last conversation that is still open.
4. Make the next step smaller than the last one proposed.
5. Set a date to stop following up, and keep to it.

## Output contract

A copy or campaign asset with a clear offer, a call to action and factual claims only.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence
- [ ] The call to action names one next step
- [ ] The asset fits the channel format

## Safety notes

Never invent statistics, testimonials or endorsements, and never make a pricing claim without approval.
