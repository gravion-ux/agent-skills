# Prepare for a long-haul flight so the journey is manageable rather than punishing

## Purpose

Prepare for a long-haul flight so the journey is manageable rather than punishing.

## When to use

Use when the user has a flight of several hours or more ahead of them.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Shift sleep earlier by small amounts over the days before departure.
2. Choose a seat with a lie-flat option if the budget allows it.
3. Plan what to do in each part of the flight, since the first meal is not enough.
4. Pack the essentials in the cabin bag, including chargers, eye mask and headphones.
5. Set a plan for arrival that accounts for jet lag rather than the clock.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
