# Prepare a travel visa application with every document checked against the official rules

## Purpose

Prepare a travel visa application with every document checked against the official rules.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to apply for a visa for travel to another country.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Open the official embassy or immigration page for the destination and the visa type.
2. List every required document with its issue date and validity period.
3. Check that the passport validity and blank-page rules are satisfied.
4. Prepare the application form and supporting evidence in the requested format.
5. Book the appointment and confirm the processing time before paying any fee.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
