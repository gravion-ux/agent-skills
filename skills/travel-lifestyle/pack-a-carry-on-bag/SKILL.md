# Pack a carry-on bag that fits the airline limits without leaving anything essential behind

## Purpose

Pack a carry-on bag that fits the airline limits without leaving anything essential behind.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to pack for a trip using only cabin baggage.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Check the airline's exact size and weight allowance for the fare type.
2. Choose the outfits by the number of days and the laundry available at the destination.
3. Pack the clothing around one colour so everything mixes.
4. Put the heavy items in the personal item and the liquids in the clear bag.
5. Carry medication, documents and one change of clothes in the cabin, not the hold.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
