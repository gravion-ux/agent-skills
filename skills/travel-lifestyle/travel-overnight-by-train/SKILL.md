# Take an overnight train with the booking, packing and arrival details planned for comfort

## Purpose

Take an overnight train with the booking, packing and arrival details planned for comfort.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to travel overnight by train and save a night's accommodation.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Choose a berth or seat type that matches how much sleep the journey needs.
2. Book a lower berth or a compartment if there is any chance of discomfort.
3. Check the departure and arrival times and the station transfer on the far side.
4. Pack for the temperature difference between the carriage and the platform.
5. Set an alarm for the arrival and confirm the station name in the local language.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
