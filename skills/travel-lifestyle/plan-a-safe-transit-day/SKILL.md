# Plan a day of moving around a city with the transfers, buffers and backups worked out

## Purpose

Plan a day of moving around a city with the transfers, buffers and backups worked out.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to get from one place to another across a day in an unfamiliar city.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. List every place the day needs and the time each one must happen.
2. Check the operating hours of the transit lines between them, including the last service.
3. Add a buffer to every connection based on how crowded the station is.
4. Write the fallback for each leg, either a different line or a taxi number.
5. Carry a transit card or the right ticket type for the whole day.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
