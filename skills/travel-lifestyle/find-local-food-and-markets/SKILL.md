# Find the local food and markets that are worth the trip without relying on tourist lists

## Purpose

Find the local food and markets that are worth the trip without relying on tourist lists.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to find somewhere genuine to eat or shop in a new place.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Find the neighbourhood where people actually shop rather than where visitors shop.
2. Check the opening days and hours of the market before planning the day around it.
3. Look for stalls with a short queue and a high turnover as a quality signal.
4. Learn the words for the key items and the prices before ordering.
5. Ask one local person for the place they would send a visitor this week.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
