# Choose travel insurance that covers the actual risks of the trip rather than the cheapest policy

## Purpose

Choose travel insurance that covers the actual risks of the trip rather than the cheapest policy.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to buy travel insurance for an upcoming trip.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. List what could actually go wrong, from cancelled flights to medical treatment abroad.
2. Check the medical cover limit against the cost of treatment in the destination.
3. Check whether pre-existing conditions are covered and what documentation is needed.
4. Compare the excess, the cancellation terms and the activities that are excluded.
5. Read the claims process and note the deadlines for reporting an incident.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
