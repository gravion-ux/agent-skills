# Plan a low-cost route that trades a little travel time for a much lower total spend

## Purpose

Plan a low-cost route that trades a little travel time for a much lower total spend.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to travel on a tight budget over a longer route.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Set the total budget and split it between transport, stay, food and activities.
2. Compare the overnight train and bus routes against the flight for each leg.
3. Choose the hubs where transport is cheapest, not the fastest overall.
4. Book the cheap legs first, because they sell out before the flexible ones.
5. Check that the longer route still fits the visa days and the user's time off.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
