# Arrange travel for a group so bookings, payments and names all stay consistent

## Purpose

Arrange travel for a group so bookings, payments and names all stay consistent.

## When to use

Use when the user is organising travel for several people at once.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Collect the names exactly as they appear on each travel document.
2. Decide who books and pays for what, and agree a shared payment deadline.
3. Book the shared parts first, such as flights and transfers, before the individual parts.
4. Share one itinerary document with times, locations and contact details.
5. Set a deadline for changes so late modifications do not split the group.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
