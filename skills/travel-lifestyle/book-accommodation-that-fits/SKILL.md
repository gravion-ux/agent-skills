# Book accommodation that fits the trip's budget, location needs and real sleeping requirements

## Purpose

Book accommodation that fits the trip's budget, location needs and real sleeping requirements.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to choose and book a place to stay.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Set the total nightly budget and the maximum distance from the areas to visit.
2. Check the room type and bed size against who is actually staying.
3. Read the reviews for noise, stairs and check-in time rather than for photos.
4. Verify the cancellation terms and the check-in window against the arrival time.
5. Confirm the total cost including taxes, resort fees and cleaning charges.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
