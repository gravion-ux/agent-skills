# Write a short travel summary that captures the trip in the voice of the person who took it

## Purpose

Write a short travel summary that captures the trip in the voice of the person who took it.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to share a short reflection about a trip they just took.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Pick one moment from the trip that actually stood out.
2. Write the feeling of that moment before writing any facts about it.
3. Add one detail only a traveller would notice, such as a sound or a smell.
4. Keep it short enough to read as a message rather than a report.
5. End with the thought the user wants to remember about the trip.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
