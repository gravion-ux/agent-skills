# Run a solo travel safety check covering documents, contacts, movement and local advice

## Purpose

Run a solo travel safety check covering documents, contacts, movement and local advice.

## When to use

Use when the user is travelling alone and wants to check they are prepared.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Verify the passport validity, the visa and the travel insurance before departure.
2. Leave the itinerary and the accommodation address with someone at home.
3. Set up the emergency contacts, the local emergency numbers and a check-in time.
4. Register with the foreign ministry travel advice service for the destination.
5. Plan what to do if the phone is lost, including how to reach the bank and the insurer.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
