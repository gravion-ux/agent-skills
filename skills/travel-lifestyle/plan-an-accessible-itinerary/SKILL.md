# Plan an accessible itinerary around step-free routes, timing and real accessibility information

## Purpose

Plan an accessible itinerary around step-free routes, timing and real accessibility information.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a trip plan that works for someone with mobility or sensory needs.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. List the access requirements for each place, such as step-free access or seating.
2. Check each venue's own accessibility information rather than relying on a review.
3. Allow extra time between places because accessible routes are often longer.
4. Check the transport between sites for lifts, gaps and the time to find them.
5. Keep a fallback for any place that turns out not to be accessible on the day.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
