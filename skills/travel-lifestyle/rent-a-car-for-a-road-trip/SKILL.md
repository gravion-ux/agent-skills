# Rent a car for a road trip with the costs, restrictions and fuel stops planned in advance

## Purpose

Rent a car for a road trip with the costs, restrictions and fuel stops planned in advance.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to hire a car for a self-drive journey.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Confirm the licence requirements and the age restrictions for the country.
2. Check whether the route crosses borders or ferry links, which usually void cover.
3. Compare the total cost including fuel, tolls, one-way fees and insurance excess.
4. Check the mileage limit and the rules for crossing onto unpaved roads.
5. Plan the fuel stops and the return-point inspection before the handover.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
