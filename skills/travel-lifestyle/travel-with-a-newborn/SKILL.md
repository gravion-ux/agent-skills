# Plan travel with a newborn around feeding, sleep, health checks and airport logistics

## Purpose

Plan travel with a newborn around feeding, sleep, health checks and airport logistics.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to travel with a very young baby.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single booking confirmation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The dates, the budget and the travellers
- The origin, destination and the constraints
- The documents, visas and booking status

## Procedure

1. Check the age rules for the airline and the destination's entry requirements for infants.
2. Confirm which health documents and vaccinations the destination needs.
3. Plan the daily rhythm around feeding and sleep rather than around sightseeing.
4. Pack the feeding, changing and medical supplies with spare copies of the essentials.
5. Write the plan for a bad day, including where to rest and how to get back to the accommodation.

## Output contract

An itinerary with timings, bookings, local logistics and a document checklist.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Times and transfer windows are feasible
- [ ] Every document requirement is checked
- [ ] The budget covers the booked items

## Safety notes

Never assume visa or entry rules are current; point the traveller to the official source and tell them to verify.
