# Turn structured information into an infographic with a clear reading order and accurate data

## Purpose

Turn structured information into an infographic with a clear reading order and accurate data.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to communicate facts, steps, or comparisons in a static visual.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a motion asset; use video-audio instead.

## Inputs required

- The subject and the intended use
- The style, aspect ratio and resolution
- The licensing and brand constraints

## Procedure

1. Extract the verified facts, units, hierarchy, and required reading order.
2. Choose a chart or diagram that accurately represents each relationship.
3. Label every section with concise text and distinguish data from commentary.
4. Apply a limited palette that keeps categories and emphasis consistent.
5. Check every number against the source and confirm labels remain legible.

## Output contract

A finished visual asset at the requested size, with its prompt or edit history recorded.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The asset matches the required dimensions
- [ ] No unlicensed or trademarked material is present
- [ ] Text in the image is legible

## Safety notes

Never create or edit an image of a real person without consent, and never reuse licensed material.
