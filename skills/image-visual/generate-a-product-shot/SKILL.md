# Create a clean product shot that presents the item accurately at the requested dimensions

## Purpose

Create a clean product shot that presents the item accurately at the requested dimensions.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a still product image for a listing, catalogue, or campaign.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a motion asset; use video-audio instead.

## Inputs required

- The subject and the intended use
- The style, aspect ratio and resolution
- The licensing and brand constraints

## Procedure

1. Record the product geometry, materials, colors, and required output dimensions.
2. Choose a lighting setup that preserves shape, surface texture, and true color.
3. Set a simple background with controlled shadows and no competing props.
4. Render the product without adding logos, labels, or accessories that were not supplied.
5. Compare the result with reference images and correct visible discrepancies.

## Output contract

A finished visual asset at the requested size, with its prompt or edit history recorded.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The asset matches the required dimensions
- [ ] No unlicensed or trademarked material is present
- [ ] Text in the image is legible

## Safety notes

Never create or edit an image of a real person without consent, and never reuse licensed material.
