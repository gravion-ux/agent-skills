# Design a social media graphic with legible hierarchy, platform dimensions, and clear brand styling

## Purpose

Design a social media graphic with legible hierarchy, platform dimensions, and clear brand styling.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a single still graphic for a social media post or campaign.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a motion asset; use video-audio instead.

## Inputs required

- The subject and the intended use
- The style, aspect ratio and resolution
- The licensing and brand constraints

## Procedure

1. Confirm the platform, canvas dimensions, safe margins, and delivery format.
2. Arrange the message into a primary headline, supporting detail, and visual focus.
3. Keep text large, high-contrast, and clear of the platform interface edges.
4. Apply approved brand colors, type, and logo placement.
5. Preview the export at feed size and check spelling, hierarchy, and legibility.

## Output contract

A finished visual asset at the requested size, with its prompt or edit history recorded.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The asset matches the required dimensions
- [ ] No unlicensed or trademarked material is present
- [ ] Text in the image is legible

## Safety notes

Never create or edit an image of a real person without consent, and never reuse licensed material.
