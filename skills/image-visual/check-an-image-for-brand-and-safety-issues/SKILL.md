# Audit an image for brand violations, unlicensed material, unsafe content, and illegible text

## Purpose

Audit an image for brand violations, unlicensed material, unsafe content, and illegible text.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a completed still image reviewed before publication or distribution.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a motion asset; use video-audio instead.

## Inputs required

- The subject and the intended use
- The style, aspect ratio and resolution
- The licensing and brand constraints

## Procedure

1. Confirm the source, permissions, consent, and brand guidelines for the asset.
2. Inspect the image for unlicensed material, trademark conflicts, and misleading claims.
3. Check depictions, language, and sensitive content against the safety policy.
4. Zoom into every text element and record any spelling, legibility, or cropping issue.
5. Return a pass-or-fail verdict with specific corrections and a retouched preview.

## Output contract

A finished visual asset at the requested size, with its prompt or edit history recorded.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The asset matches the required dimensions
- [ ] No unlicensed or trademarked material is present
- [ ] Text in the image is legible

## Safety notes

Never create or edit an image of a real person without consent, and never reuse licensed material.
