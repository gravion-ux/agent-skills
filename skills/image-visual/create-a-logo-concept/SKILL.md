# Develop a logo concept with a clear silhouette and a documented rights check

## Purpose

Develop a logo concept with a clear silhouette and a documented rights check.

## When to use

Use when the user needs an original still logo concept for brand exploration.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a motion asset; use video-audio instead.

## Inputs required

- The subject and the intended use
- The style, aspect ratio and resolution
- The licensing and brand constraints

## Procedure

1. Capture the brand audience, message, required uses, and prohibited elements.
2. Sketch three distinct concepts centered on simple, recognizable geometry.
3. Test each concept in one-color, reversed, and small-size treatments.
4. Record color, spacing, and clear-space guidance for the chosen direction.
5. Search the final mark for visual similarity and trademark conflicts before presentation.

## Output contract

A finished visual asset at the requested size, with its prompt or edit history recorded.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The asset matches the required dimensions
- [ ] No unlicensed or trademarked material is present
- [ ] Text in the image is legible

## Safety notes

Never create or edit an image of a real person without consent, and never reuse licensed material.
