# Generate a storyboard frame that communicates a scene's action, framing, and shot intent

## Purpose

Generate a storyboard frame that communicates a scene's action, framing, and shot intent.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a still storyboard frame for planning a sequence or production.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a motion asset; use video-audio instead.

## Inputs required

- The subject and the intended use
- The style, aspect ratio and resolution
- The licensing and brand constraints

## Procedure

1. Record the shot number, narrative beat, camera framing, and action in the scene.
2. Describe subject placement, gaze direction, gesture, setting, and focal emphasis.
3. Use a simple visual style that supports planning rather than final rendering.
4. Keep continuity details consistent with adjacent storyboard frames.
5. Label the frame with its shot number and save it beside the shot list.

## Output contract

A finished visual asset at the requested size, with its prompt or edit history recorded.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The asset matches the required dimensions
- [ ] No unlicensed or trademarked material is present
- [ ] Text in the image is legible

## Safety notes

Never create or edit an image of a real person without consent, and never reuse licensed material.
