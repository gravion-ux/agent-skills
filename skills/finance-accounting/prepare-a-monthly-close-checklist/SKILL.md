# Prepare a monthly close checklist that lists every step, owner and dependency in order

## Purpose

Prepare a monthly close checklist that lists every step, owner and dependency in order.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a repeatable checklist for closing the books each month.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a budget spreadsheet for a small team; use personal-finance instead.

## Inputs required

- The transactions, invoices or ledger in scope
- The accounting rules and the reporting period
- The reconciliation tolerance

## Procedure

1. List the close tasks in the order they must happen, not in the order people think of them.
2. Name the owner and the reviewer for each task.
3. Mark which tasks depend on a reconciliation or an approval from another team.
4. Put the cut-off dates next to each task so late inputs are visible early.
5. Record the completion evidence each task must leave behind.

## Output contract

A reconciled ledger or report with every difference explained and flagged.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The totals tie to the source records
- [ ] Every adjustment is traceable to a rule or an approval
- [ ] Rounding differences are within tolerance

## Safety notes

Never post, approve or pay a transaction without explicit authorisation, and never alter a closed period.
