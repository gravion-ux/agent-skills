# Prepare a board financial summary that leads with what changed and what needs a decision

## Purpose

Prepare a board financial summary that leads with what changed and what needs a decision.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a short financial summary for a board or leadership meeting.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a budget spreadsheet for a small team; use personal-finance instead.

## Inputs required

- The transactions, invoices or ledger in scope
- The accounting rules and the reporting period
- The reconciliation tolerance

## Procedure

1. Write the headline result in one sentence with the comparison to the prior period.
2. Show the three numbers that moved most and explain each in a sentence.
3. State the cash position and the forecast balance at the next key date.
4. List the decisions the board needs to make, with a recommendation for each.
5. Keep the detailed schedules in an appendix rather than in the summary itself.

## Output contract

A reconciled ledger or report with every difference explained and flagged.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The totals tie to the source records
- [ ] Every adjustment is traceable to a rule or an approval
- [ ] Rounding differences are within tolerance

## Safety notes

Never post, approve or pay a transaction without explicit authorisation, and never alter a closed period.
