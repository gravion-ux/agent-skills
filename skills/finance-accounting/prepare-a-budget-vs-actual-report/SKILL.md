# Prepare a budget versus actual report that highlights the variances worth acting on

## Purpose

Prepare a budget versus actual report that highlights the variances worth acting on.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a report comparing the budget with the actual results.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a budget spreadsheet for a small team; use personal-finance instead.

## Inputs required

- The transactions, invoices or ledger in scope
- The accounting rules and the reporting period
- The reconciliation tolerance

## Procedure

1. Compare the same period in both sets of figures, not a full year against a month.
2. Separate the variance caused by timing from the variance caused by a real difference.
3. Rank the variances by their effect on the result rather than by size alone.
4. Ask the owner of each large variance for the reason and the expected recovery.
5. Write the action and the owner next to every variance that will not correct itself.

## Output contract

A reconciled ledger or report with every difference explained and flagged.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The totals tie to the source records
- [ ] Every adjustment is traceable to a rule or an approval
- [ ] Rounding differences are within tolerance

## Safety notes

Never post, approve or pay a transaction without explicit authorisation, and never alter a closed period.
