# Explain a profit and loss statement by tracing each material movement to its cause

## Purpose

Explain a profit and loss statement by tracing each material movement to its cause.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to understand what drove the profit or loss in a period.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a budget spreadsheet for a small team; use personal-finance instead.

## Inputs required

- The transactions, invoices or ledger in scope
- The accounting rules and the reporting period
- The reconciliation tolerance

## Procedure

1. Start from the net result and work backwards to the largest drivers of the movement.
2. Separate the movement caused by volume from the movement caused by price or mix.
3. Check that the one-off items are flagged and excluded from the run-rate figure.
4. Compare the margin with the prior period and with the plan.
5. Write the three sentences that explain the result to someone who has not seen the numbers.

## Output contract

A reconciled ledger or report with every difference explained and flagged.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The totals tie to the source records
- [ ] Every adjustment is traceable to a rule or an approval
- [ ] Rounding differences are within tolerance

## Safety notes

Never post, approve or pay a transaction without explicit authorisation, and never alter a closed period.
