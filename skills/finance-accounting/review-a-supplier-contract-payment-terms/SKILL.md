# Review a supplier contract's payment terms for cost, risk and admin burden

## Purpose

Review a supplier contract's payment terms for cost, risk and admin burden.

## When to use

Use when the user wants to check the payment terms in a supplier contract before signing.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a budget spreadsheet for a small team; use personal-finance instead.

## Inputs required

- The transactions, invoices or ledger in scope
- The accounting rules and the reporting period
- The reconciliation tolerance

## Procedure

1. Extract the payment terms, the due dates and any early settlement discount.
2. Calculate the true cost of the terms against the business's cash position.
3. Check the late payment consequences, the suspension rights and the price increase clauses.
4. Compare the terms with the business's standard terms and the supplier's other contracts.
5. Negotiate the specific clauses that carry the most risk and record the agreed wording.

## Output contract

A reconciled ledger or report with every difference explained and flagged.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The totals tie to the source records
- [ ] Every adjustment is traceable to a rule or an approval
- [ ] Rounding differences are within tolerance

## Safety notes

Never post, approve or pay a transaction without explicit authorisation, and never alter a closed period.
