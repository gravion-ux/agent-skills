# Plan a proof of concept with a specific hypothesis, success threshold, scope boundary, and decision date

## Purpose

Plan a proof of concept with a specific hypothesis, success threshold, scope boundary, and decision date.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to test a technical or product idea before committing to a full project.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for the technical design of the work; use architecture-design instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal, deadline and constraints
- The people and their capacity
- The dependencies outside the team's control

## Procedure

1. State the hypothesis, user problem, and decision the proof of concept will enable.
2. Define the smallest test scope, test data, environment, and time box.
3. Set quantitative success and failure thresholds before running the test.
4. Assign work, isolate risks, and record evidence against each acceptance check.
5. Schedule the review and document the invest, revise, or stop decision.

## Output contract

A plan with milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and a status view that stays true.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every task has one owner
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are visible
- [ ] The plan reflects actual status, not the original estimate

## Safety notes

Never report progress that is not backed by delivered work.
