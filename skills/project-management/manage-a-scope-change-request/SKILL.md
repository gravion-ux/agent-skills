# Evaluate a scope change against value, capacity, dependencies, and impact before approving it

## Purpose

Evaluate a scope change against value, capacity, dependencies, and impact before approving it.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to assess a proposed change to committed project scope.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for the technical design of the work; use architecture-design instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal, deadline and constraints
- The people and their capacity
- The dependencies outside the team's control

## Procedure

1. Document the requested change, its purpose, and the acceptance criteria.
2. Identify affected deliverables, dependencies, milestones, and owners.
3. Estimate the effort, trade-offs, and capacity impact with the affected owners.
4. Compare benefits and risks against the current plan and alternatives.
5. Record the approval decision, rejected impacts, and required plan updates.

## Output contract

A plan with milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and a status view that stays true.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every task has one owner
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are visible
- [ ] The plan reflects actual status, not the original estimate

## Safety notes

Never report progress that is not backed by delivered work.
