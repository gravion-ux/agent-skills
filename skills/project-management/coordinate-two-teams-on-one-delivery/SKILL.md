# Coordinate two teams on one delivery through a shared interface, explicit handoffs, and joint verification

## Purpose

Coordinate two teams on one delivery through a shared interface, explicit handoffs, and joint verification.

## When to use

Use when the user needs two teams to deliver one coordinated outcome without ownership gaps.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for the technical design of the work; use architecture-design instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal, deadline and constraints
- The people and their capacity
- The dependencies outside the team's control

## Procedure

1. Define the shared outcome, interface artefacts, and acceptance criteria.
2. Assign one accountable owner in each team for its interface and commitments.
3. Map handoffs, dependencies, blockers, and response deadlines between teams.
4. Schedule joint checkpoints and a final integration verification.
5. Report completion only for work verified across both team boundaries.

## Output contract

A plan with milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and a status view that stays true.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every task has one owner
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are visible
- [ ] The plan reflects actual status, not the original estimate

## Safety notes

Never report progress that is not backed by delivered work.
