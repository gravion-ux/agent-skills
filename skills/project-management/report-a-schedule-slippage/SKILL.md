# Report a schedule slippage with evidence, affected milestones, causes, and a credible recovery plan

## Purpose

Report a schedule slippage with evidence, affected milestones, causes, and a credible recovery plan.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to communicate a delayed project schedule to stakeholders.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for the technical design of the work; use architecture-design instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal, deadline and constraints
- The people and their capacity
- The dependencies outside the team's control

## Procedure

1. Confirm the delay against the current baseline schedule and delivered work.
2. Quantify the variance in days and identify every affected milestone.
3. Separate confirmed causes from unverified explanations.
4. Document a recovery plan with owners, actions, and target dates.
5. State the impact and request decisions needed from stakeholders.

## Output contract

A plan with milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and a status view that stays true.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every task has one owner
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are visible
- [ ] The plan reflects actual status, not the original estimate

## Safety notes

Never report progress that is not backed by delivered work.
