# Close a project with acceptance evidence, outcome assessment, handover, and reusable lessons learned

## Purpose

Close a project with acceptance evidence, outcome assessment, handover, and reusable lessons learned.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to close a completed project and preserve its final records.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for the technical design of the work; use architecture-design instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal, deadline and constraints
- The people and their capacity
- The dependencies outside the team's control

## Procedure

1. Verify the agreed deliverables against acceptance criteria and record evidence.
2. Document the outcome, benefits realized, unresolved items, and owners for follow-up.
3. Release resources and transfer ongoing work, assets, access, and obligations.
4. Facilitate a lessons session covering decisions, outcomes, and improvement actions.
5. Archive the plan, status history, risk register, and final accountability record.

## Output contract

A plan with milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and a status view that stays true.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every task has one owner
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are visible
- [ ] The plan reflects actual status, not the original estimate

## Safety notes

Never report progress that is not backed by delivered work.
