# Maintain a project risk register with scored exposure, owners, responses, and review dates

## Purpose

Maintain a project risk register with scored exposure, owners, responses, and review dates.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a repeatable process for keeping project risks current.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for the technical design of the work; use architecture-design instead.

## Inputs required

- The goal, deadline and constraints
- The people and their capacity
- The dependencies outside the team's control

## Procedure

1. Collect risks from workstream owners and dependency boundaries.
2. Record each risk's cause, event, impact, probability, and response owner.
3. Score exposure consistently and prioritize the highest-rated items.
4. Attach mitigation actions, due dates, indicators, and escalation thresholds.
5. Review the register against delivered work and archive resolved risks.

## Output contract

A plan with milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and a status view that stays true.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every task has one owner
- [ ] Dependencies and blockers are visible
- [ ] The plan reflects actual status, not the original estimate

## Safety notes

Never report progress that is not backed by delivered work.
