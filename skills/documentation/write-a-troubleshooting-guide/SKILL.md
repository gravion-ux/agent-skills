# Diagnose common failures with ordered checks, causes, fixes, and escalation cues

## Purpose

Diagnose common failures with ordered checks, causes, fixes, and escalation cues.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a troubleshooting guide for recurring product or service failures.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a persuasive article or campaign copy; use content-creation instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and what they must be able to do
- The subject and the source material
- The format and the maintenance owner

## Procedure

1. Record the symptom, environment, and exact error message.
2. Order checks from the least disruptive diagnostic to the most invasive.
3. Map each check to a cause and a concrete fix.
4. Test the fix and note the result that confirms success.
5. State when to stop troubleshooting and escalate the issue.

## Output contract

A document that answers the reader's question, with structure, examples and an owner.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The document answers its stated question
- [ ] Examples are runnable and current
- [ ] Every claim can be traced to a source

## Safety notes

Never document a command you have not run, and never leave an internal secret in an example.
