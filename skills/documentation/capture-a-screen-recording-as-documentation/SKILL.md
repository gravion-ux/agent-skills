# Turn a product recording into a focused guide with captions, steps, and checkpoints

## Purpose

Turn a product recording into a focused guide with captions, steps, and checkpoints.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a screen recording to serve as usable product documentation.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a persuasive article or campaign copy; use content-creation instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and what they must be able to do
- The subject and the source material
- The format and the maintenance owner

## Procedure

1. Define the single task the recording must help a reader complete.
2. Script each visible action and prepare a clean demonstration environment.
3. Record the full flow without showing credentials or private data.
4. Add captions that name each action and its expected result.
5. Verify recorded commands separately and provide a transcript fallback.

## Output contract

A document that answers the reader's question, with structure, examples and an owner.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The document answers its stated question
- [ ] Examples are runnable and current
- [ ] Every claim can be traced to a source

## Safety notes

Never document a command you have not run, and never leave an internal secret in an example.
