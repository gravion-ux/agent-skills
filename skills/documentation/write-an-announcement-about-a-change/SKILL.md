# Announce a change with impact, timing, action required, and a source of truth

## Purpose

Announce a change with impact, timing, action required, and a source of truth.

## When to use

Use when the user wants an announcement that tells customers about a product or policy change.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a persuasive article or campaign copy; use content-creation instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and what they must be able to do
- The subject and the source material
- The format and the maintenance owner

## Procedure

1. Identify the audience, affected behavior, and effective date.
2. State the change in plain language before providing background.
3. Specify the action readers must take and when they must take it.
4. Link to the authoritative details and the support contact.
5. Have the change owner verify the announcement against the release.

## Output contract

A document that answers the reader's question, with structure, examples and an owner.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The document answers its stated question
- [ ] Examples are runnable and current
- [ ] Every claim can be traced to a source

## Safety notes

Never document a command you have not run, and never leave an internal secret in an example.
