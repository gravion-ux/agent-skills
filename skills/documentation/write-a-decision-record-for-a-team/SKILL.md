# Capture a team decision, its context, alternatives, consequences, and review date

## Purpose

Capture a team decision, its context, alternatives, consequences, and review date.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a durable record of why a technical or product decision was made.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a persuasive article or campaign copy; use content-creation instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and what they must be able to do
- The subject and the source material
- The format and the maintenance owner

## Procedure

1. State the problem, constraints, and forces that shaped the decision.
2. List the considered options with their material trade-offs.
3. Record the chosen option and the reason for selecting it.
4. Describe the expected consequences, risks, and follow-up actions.
5. Name the decision owner and the date for revisiting it.

## Output contract

A document that answers the reader's question, with structure, examples and an owner.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The document answers its stated question
- [ ] Examples are runnable and current
- [ ] Every claim can be traced to a source

## Safety notes

Never document a command you have not run, and never leave an internal secret in an example.
