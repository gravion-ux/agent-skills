# Answer recurring questions with concise, sourced responses and clear escalation paths

## Purpose

Answer recurring questions with concise, sourced responses and clear escalation paths.

## When to use

Use when the user wants a FAQ that resolves common questions about a product or service.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a persuasive article or campaign copy; use content-creation instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and what they must be able to do
- The subject and the source material
- The format and the maintenance owner

## Procedure

1. Collect questions from support tickets, community posts, and usage data.
2. Group related questions and remove duplicates.
3. Answer each question directly in the first sentence.
4. Cite the authoritative source and add an escalation route when needed.
5. Have the maintenance owner verify every answer.

## Output contract

A document that answers the reader's question, with structure, examples and an owner.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The document answers its stated question
- [ ] Examples are runnable and current
- [ ] Every claim can be traced to a source

## Safety notes

Never document a command you have not run, and never leave an internal secret in an example.
