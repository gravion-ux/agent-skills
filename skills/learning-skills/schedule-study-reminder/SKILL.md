# Schedule study reminders that match the learner's time and review rhythm

## Purpose

Schedule study reminders that match the learner's time and review rhythm.

## When to use

Use when the user wants recurring reminders for study and practice sessions.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for producing a training artefact for a team; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner's level and goal
- The time available
- The practice or assessment that proves learning

## Procedure

1. Collect the learner's available days and times.
2. Choose reminders tied to each study session.
3. Space the reminders around planned review intervals.
4. Keep each reminder short and action-focused.
5. Adjust the schedule after the first week.

## Output contract

A study plan with exercises, a checkpoint schedule and a self-test.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan has a measurable outcome
- [ ] Practice starts before the reading ends
- [ ] There is a way to check retention

## Safety notes

Never present unverified claims about a topic as established fact.
