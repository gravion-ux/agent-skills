# Delegate a task with an explicit outcome, owner, guardrails, and acceptance check

## Purpose

Delegate a task with an explicit outcome, owner, guardrails, and acceptance check.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to hand work to another person or agent.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Describe the final deliverable and define what completion looks like.
2. Assign one owner and record the deadline and decision authority.
3. Specify required inputs, constraints, and the approval needed for sensitive changes.
4. Choose a check that tests the deliverable against the requested outcome.
5. Schedule a review and transfer ownership of unresolved follow-up.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
