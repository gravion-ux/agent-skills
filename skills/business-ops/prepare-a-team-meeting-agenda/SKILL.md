# Prepare a focused team meeting agenda with objectives, owners, and timed discussion items

## Purpose

Prepare a focused team meeting agenda with objectives, owners, and timed discussion items.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to organize a structured team meeting.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. State the meeting objective and the decision or output required.
2. Collect candidate topics from the owner and attendees.
3. Prioritize topics by impact and assign one discussion owner to each.
4. Assign a time box and required pre-reading to every agenda item.
5. Define the meeting success metric and its review date.
6. End with the decisions, actions, and next checkpoint the meeting must produce.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
