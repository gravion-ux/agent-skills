# Build a business case for a project with value, costs, risks, and approval criteria

## Purpose

Build a business case for a project with value, costs, risks, and approval criteria.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to justify a project investment before a funding decision.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Define the business problem, proposed project, scope, owner, and decision deadline.
2. Quantify the baseline, expected benefits, costs, timing, and confidence in each estimate.
3. Compare alternatives and test whether the value remains positive under adverse assumptions.
4. Identify delivery, legal, financial, data, and operational risks with mitigation owners.
5. State the recommendation, required approvals, funding limit, and measurable outcome.
6. Attach the evidence model and schedule a post-implementation benefits review.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
