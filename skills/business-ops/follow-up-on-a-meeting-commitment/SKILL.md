# Close a meeting commitment by confirming the owner, deliverable, and evidence of completion

## Purpose

Close a meeting commitment by confirming the owner, deliverable, and evidence of completion.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to ensure an agreed meeting action is completed.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Retrieve the commitment, promised output, owner, and original deadline from the minutes.
2. Ask the owner to confirm the deliverable and identify any blocker.
3. Route financial, legal, or contractual changes to the accountable owner for approval.
4. Send one consolidated reminder listing the required evidence.
5. Record completion evidence and close, reschedule, or escalate the commitment.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
