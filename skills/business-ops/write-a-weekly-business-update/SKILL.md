# Compile a weekly business update with progress, risks, decisions, and next priorities

## Purpose

Compile a weekly business update with progress, risks, decisions, and next priorities.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a concise status update for business stakeholders.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Collect each workstream's progress, metrics, risks, and decisions since the last update.
2. Attach a headline metric and its target to each workstream.
3. Name an owner and due date for every risk or unresolved dependency.
4. Separate confirmed results from forecasts and unverified assumptions.
5. Group the content under results, risks, decisions, and next priorities.
6. Publish the update and retain review comments for the next cycle.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
