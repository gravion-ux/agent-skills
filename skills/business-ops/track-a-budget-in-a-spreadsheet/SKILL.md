# Track a budget in a spreadsheet with clear categories, owners, variances, and thresholds

## Purpose

Track a budget in a spreadsheet with clear categories, owners, variances, and thresholds.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to maintain an operational budget in a spreadsheet.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Define the budget period, approved total, categories, owners, and coding rules.
2. Record actual and committed amounts with dates, sources, and category labels.
3. Calculate variances against budget and flag any threshold breach for review.
4. Reconcile changes against the approved baseline before updating forecasts.
5. Protect formulas and source data, and obtain owner approval before changing totals.
6. Publish a summary that shows spend rate, variance, and forecast at period end.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
