# Write meeting minutes that capture decisions, owners, deadlines, and open questions

## Purpose

Write meeting minutes that capture decisions, owners, deadlines, and open questions.

## When to use

Use when the user needs accurate minutes and accountable follow-up from a meeting.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Record the meeting date, attendees, objective, and facilitator.
2. Capture each decision with its rationale and any approved financial or legal condition.
3. Assign one owner and a due date to every action item.
4. Link each action to the metric that will show completion.
5. List unresolved questions with the person responsible for answering them.
6. Distribute the minutes promptly and record corrections to the agreed record.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
