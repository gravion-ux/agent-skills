# Prepare a decision brief with options, evidence, trade-offs, risks, and a clear recommendation

## Purpose

Prepare a decision brief with options, evidence, trade-offs, risks, and a clear recommendation.

## When to use

Use when the user needs decision-makers to evaluate a consequential business choice.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. State the decision, deadline, accountable owner, and criteria for choosing.
2. Define the options and the baseline against which they will be compared.
3. Present evidence for each option with source, uncertainty, and cost or impact.
4. Surface risks, dependencies, reversibility, and any required approvals.
5. Mark financial, legal, and contractual assumptions for owner approval.
6. Recommend one option with rationale and name the metric that will evaluate the choice.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
