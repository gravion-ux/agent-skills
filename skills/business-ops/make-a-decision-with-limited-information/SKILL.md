# Make a reversible decision under incomplete information by testing assumptions and defining a checkpoint

## Purpose

Make a reversible decision under incomplete information by testing assumptions and defining a checkpoint.

## When to use

Use when the user must choose a course of action before all information is available.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Frame the decision, deadline, options, and minimum acceptable outcome.
2. List known facts, assumptions, and the unknown information most likely to change the choice.
3. Assign confidence levels and identify which assumptions are safe to test cheaply.
4. Choose the option that limits downside while preserving a future path.
5. Record the decision, rationale, owner, and the metric that will show whether it worked.
6. Schedule a review when new evidence could justify reversing the choice.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
