# Summarize a meeting for a stakeholder with decisions, impact, actions, and open risks

## Purpose

Summarize a meeting for a stakeholder with decisions, impact, actions, and open risks.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a stakeholder-focused summary rather than full meeting minutes.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Identify the stakeholder's decisions, interests, and information gaps.
2. Extract only outcomes that affect the stakeholder's scope or decisions.
3. State each conclusion, implication, and dependency without unsupported inference.
4. Name the owner and due date for actions requiring stakeholder awareness.
5. Flag unresolved risks, approval requests, and the next update date.
6. Deliver the summary promptly and record acknowledgements of key decisions.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
