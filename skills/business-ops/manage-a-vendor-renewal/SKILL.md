# Manage a vendor renewal with performance review, open issues, negotiation, and approval

## Purpose

Manage a vendor renewal with performance review, open issues, negotiation, and approval.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to prepare or decide a time-bound vendor renewal.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Confirm the renewal date, notice period, contract owner, and current service levels.
2. Review performance, incidents, spend, open issues, and competitor or market evidence.
3. Decide whether to renew, renegotiate, reduce scope, or exit, with supporting evidence.
4. Route proposed price, legal, data, and contract changes to the accountable owners.
5. Record the approved decision, final terms, obligations, and renewal calendar.
6. Confirm service transition ownership and the metric for the next contract term.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
