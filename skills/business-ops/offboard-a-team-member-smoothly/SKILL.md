# Coordinate a team member's offboarding with controlled access, handover, and closeout

## Purpose

Coordinate a team member's offboarding with controlled access, handover, and closeout.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to offboard a departing team member without operational gaps.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Confirm the departure scope, effective time, and authorized owner of the request.
2. Record access changes, asset returns, handover owners, and knowledge-transfer tasks.
3. Route legal, contractual, payroll, and benefits steps to their accountable owners.
4. Revoke access only at the authorized time and preserve required records.
5. Complete handover acceptance and record any residual access or support risk.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
