# Check a process execution against required controls and document exceptions with owners

## Purpose

Check a process execution against required controls and document exceptions with owners.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to verify that a process followed its documented controls.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Collect the procedure version, execution evidence, and applicable control requirements.
2. Test each step against its owner, required input, output, and approval evidence.
3. Classify each deviation by severity and potential operational impact.
4. Assign an owner and correction deadline to every material exception.
5. Record the compliance rate, unresolved exceptions, and required attestation.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
