# Onboard a new hire with role context, access, stakeholders, and first measurable work

## Purpose

Onboard a new hire with role context, access, stakeholders, and first measurable work.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to bring a new team member into business operations safely.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Document the role's purpose, recurring processes, service levels, and key contacts.
2. Assign a buddy and owners for access, equipment, policies, and orientation.
3. Schedule learning in the order required by the person's first operational tasks.
4. Run a supervised walkthrough of one safe, low-risk workflow.
5. Set a 30-day check with a task, a metric, and an escalation path.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
