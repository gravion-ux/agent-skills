# Forecast next-quarter operating costs with drivers, scenarios, and a variance-based update path

## Purpose

Forecast next-quarter operating costs with drivers, scenarios, and a variance-based update path.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a forward estimate of business operating costs.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Collect actual costs for recent months, committed spend, contracts, and planned activity.
2. Break each cost into a driver, rate, volume, timing, and confidence level.
3. Model baseline, expected, and adverse scenarios without mixing one-time and recurring items.
4. Check payroll, tax, regulatory, and contractual assumptions with their owners.
5. Compare the forecast with the approved budget and explain material variances.
6. Set monthly update triggers and record the forecast metric and variance threshold.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
