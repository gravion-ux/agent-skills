# Run a vendor selection check against requirements, risk, cost, and contract conditions

## Purpose

Run a vendor selection check against requirements, risk, cost, and contract conditions.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to evaluate a vendor against operational and procurement criteria.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Define must-have requirements, service levels, budget limits, and evaluation weights.
2. Collect comparable quotes, references, security evidence, and service details.
3. Score each vendor consistently and document gaps or assumptions in the evidence.
4. Assess financial, legal, data, and contractual risks with the accountable owners.
5. Recommend a vendor only after required owner approvals and record the rationale.
6. Set contract, service, review, and renewal dates once the choice is approved.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
