# Prepare a budget proposal with assumptions, costs, options, controls, and approval needs

## Purpose

Prepare a budget proposal with assumptions, costs, options, controls, and approval needs.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to request funding or an allocation change for a business period.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. State the funding objective, scope, period, baseline, and accountable owner.
2. Estimate revenues, operating costs, one-time costs, and timing assumptions with sources.
3. Model conservative, expected, and upside scenarios and identify the cost drivers.
4. Define spending controls, reporting cadence, and the metrics for accountability.
5. Present trade-offs and alternatives, and route financial commitments for owner approval.
6. Attach the approval record and final approved budget to the request.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
