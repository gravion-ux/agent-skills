# Document a business process with owners, inputs, steps, exceptions, and success measures

## Purpose

Document a business process with owners, inputs, steps, exceptions, and success measures.

## When to use

Use when the user needs a repeatable record of how a business process runs.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Define the trigger, completion point, owner, and expected service level.
2. List every input, supplier, and receiving role at the handoff points.
3. Write the ordered steps and identify the artefact produced by each step.
4. Document the failure path, escalation rule, and recovery owner.
5. Name the metric that proves the process works and schedule a review.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
