# Collect and organize business feedback into themes, evidence, owners, and response decisions

## Purpose

Collect and organize business feedback into themes, evidence, owners, and response decisions.

## When to use

Use when the user needs to turn scattered stakeholder feedback into actionable business insight.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a single customer conversation; use communication instead.

## Inputs required

- The process being run
- The inputs and who supplies them
- The service level the process must meet

## Procedure

1. Define the feedback question, audience, collection window, and response format.
2. Gather responses with source, date, segment, and verbatim supporting detail.
3. Group related comments into themes and note contradictory evidence.
4. Score each theme by reach, impact, and confidence using the supplied criteria.
5. Assign an owner and response decision for each theme worth acting on.
6. Publish the evidence table and track which decisions were accepted or deferred.

## Output contract

A documented operating procedure with owners, inputs, steps and the metrics it is judged on.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every step has an owner
- [ ] The failure path is documented
- [ ] The metric that proves the process works is named

## Safety notes

Never change a financial, legal or contractual step without the owner's approval.
