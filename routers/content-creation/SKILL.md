# Content creation router

## Purpose

List every leaf skill in the content-creation domain and say when to prefer each one, so the master skill can pick a chain without loading the whole catalogue.

## When to use

Use when the task is clearly inside content-creation and the master skill needs the structural neighbours of a shortlisted skill.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for internal engineering documentation; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The platform and the format
- The audience and the tone
- The source material, the hook and the call to action

## Procedure

1. Read the task and the domain framing above.
2. Scan the skill table below and shortlist at most five candidates.
3. Drop any candidate whose When NOT to use clause rules it out.
4. Order the survivors from most specific to most general.
5. Recommend one primary skill and at most three supporting skills.

## Output contract

A publishable draft in the target format, with a title, hook, body and close.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The opening earns the next sentence
- [ ] The draft matches the platform's format and length
- [ ] Every factual claim is sourced

## Safety notes

Never publish without approval, and never present generated statistics as real data.
