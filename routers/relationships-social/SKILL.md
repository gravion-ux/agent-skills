# Relationships and social skills router

## Purpose

List every leaf skill in the relationships-social domain and say when to prefer each one, so the master skill can pick a chain without loading the whole catalogue.

## When to use

Use when the task is clearly inside relationships-social and the master skill needs the structural neighbours of a shortlisted skill.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a workplace conflict that needs a formal process; use communication or business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The situation and the relationship
- What the user wants to happen next
- The tone and the boundaries that apply

## Procedure

1. Read the task and the domain framing above.
2. Scan the skill table below and shortlist at most five candidates.
3. Drop any candidate whose When NOT to use clause rules it out.
4. Order the survivors from most specific to most general.
5. Recommend one primary skill and at most three supporting skills.

## Output contract

A suggested approach with wording the user can adapt, plus the outcome to watch for.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The tone matches the relationship
- [ ] The ask is reasonable and specific
- [ ] The user can edit before sending

## Safety notes

Never send the message without approval, and never advise on a situation where the user is at risk of harm.
