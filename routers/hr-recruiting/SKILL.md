# HR and recruiting router

## Purpose

List every leaf skill in the hr-recruiting domain and say when to prefer each one, so the master skill can pick a chain without loading the whole catalogue.

## When to use

Use when the task is clearly inside hr-recruiting and the master skill needs the structural neighbours of a shortlisted skill.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for an onboarding checklist for an existing employee; use business-ops instead.

## Inputs required

- The role, the team and the hiring stage
- The candidate material in scope
- The evaluation criteria and the decision timeline

## Procedure

1. Read the task and the domain framing above.
2. Scan the skill table below and shortlist at most five candidates.
3. Drop any candidate whose When NOT to use clause rules it out.
4. Order the survivors from most specific to most general.
5. Recommend one primary skill and at most three supporting skills.

## Output contract

A structured evaluation or hiring artefact that applies the stated criteria consistently.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every criterion is applied to every candidate
- [ ] The decision is traceable to the criteria
- [ ] Personal data is limited to what the process needs

## Safety notes

Never make a hiring decision without human review, and never infer protected attributes from a CV.
