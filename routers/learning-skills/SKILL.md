# Learning and study router

## Purpose

List every leaf skill in the learning-skills domain and say when to prefer each one, so the master skill can pick a chain without loading the whole catalogue.

## When to use

Use when the task is clearly inside learning-skills and the master skill needs the structural neighbours of a shortlisted skill.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for producing a training artefact for a team; use technical-writing instead.

## Inputs required

- The learner's level and goal
- The time available
- The practice or assessment that proves learning

## Procedure

1. Read the task and the domain framing above.
2. Scan the skill table below and shortlist at most five candidates.
3. Drop any candidate whose When NOT to use clause rules it out.
4. Order the survivors from most specific to most general.
5. Recommend one primary skill and at most three supporting skills.

## Output contract

A study plan with exercises, a checkpoint schedule and a self-test.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The plan has a measurable outcome
- [ ] Practice starts before the reading ends
- [ ] There is a way to check retention

## Safety notes

Never present unverified claims about a topic as established fact.
