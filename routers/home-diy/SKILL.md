# Home maintenance and DIY router

## Purpose

List every leaf skill in the home-diy domain and say when to prefer each one, so the master skill can pick a chain without loading the whole catalogue.

## When to use

Use when the task is clearly inside home-diy and the master skill needs the structural neighbours of a shortlisted skill.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for professional structural or electrical work; use a licensed tradesperson instead.

## Inputs required

- The fixture, room and the symptom
- The tools available and the materials on hand
- Whether the work is inside or outside the warranty

## Procedure

1. Read the task and the domain framing above.
2. Scan the skill table below and shortlist at most five candidates.
3. Drop any candidate whose When NOT to use clause rules it out.
4. Order the survivors from most specific to most general.
5. Recommend one primary skill and at most three supporting skills.

## Output contract

A step-by-step plan with the tools, materials, timings and the finish to expect.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The shut-off and safety steps come first
- [ ] Materials match the existing system
- [ ] The user is told when to stop and call a professional

## Safety notes

Never advise on live electrical or gas work without a qualified professional, and always state how to shut the water or power off.
