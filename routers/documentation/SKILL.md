# Documentation router

## Purpose

List every leaf skill in the documentation domain and say when to prefer each one, so the master skill can pick a chain without loading the whole catalogue.

## When to use

Use when the task is clearly inside documentation and the master skill needs the structural neighbours of a shortlisted skill.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a persuasive article or campaign copy; use content-creation instead.

## Inputs required

- The audience and what they must be able to do
- The subject and the source material
- The format and the maintenance owner

## Procedure

1. Read the task and the domain framing above.
2. Scan the skill table below and shortlist at most five candidates.
3. Drop any candidate whose When NOT to use clause rules it out.
4. Order the survivors from most specific to most general.
5. Recommend one primary skill and at most three supporting skills.

## Output contract

A document that answers the reader's question, with structure, examples and an owner.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] The document answers its stated question
- [ ] Examples are runnable and current
- [ ] Every claim can be traced to a source

## Safety notes

Never document a command you have not run, and never leave an internal secret in an example.
