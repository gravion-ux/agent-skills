# Legal and compliance router

## Purpose

List every leaf skill in the legal-compliance domain and say when to prefer each one, so the master skill can pick a chain without loading the whole catalogue.

## When to use

Use when the task is clearly inside legal-compliance and the master skill needs the structural neighbours of a shortlisted skill.

## When NOT to use

Do not use this for a routine internal policy note; use documentation instead.

## Inputs required

- The document, process or data in scope
- The jurisdiction and the obligations
- The deadline and the risk of non-compliance

## Procedure

1. Read the task and the domain framing above.
2. Scan the skill table below and shortlist at most five candidates.
3. Drop any candidate whose When NOT to use clause rules it out.
4. Order the survivors from most specific to most general.
5. Recommend one primary skill and at most three supporting skills.

## Output contract

A review with the obligations mapped, the gaps listed and a recommendation per gap.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every requirement is traced to a source clause
- [ ] Gaps are ranked by severity
- [ ] The output states that it is not legal advice

## Safety notes

Never present the output as legal advice, and never sign, file or publish on the user's behalf.
